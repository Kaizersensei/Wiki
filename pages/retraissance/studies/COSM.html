Semi-Passive Creator Revenue Channels

Objective: Create once → sell many (books/comics, PDFs, asset packs, merch).
Constraints: free to make first sales, no mandatory subscription, manageable by one assigned operator.

Scope: Channel discovery + free-tier requirements/capabilities only. Strategy comes after initial selection.

1) Digital-First Publishing (Lowest friction)

Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing)

Best for: ebooks, print-on-demand paperbacks/hardcovers (lore books, guides, comics volumes, artbooks).

  • Cost: free to publish; no subscription required.
  • Free capabilities: upload ebooks + paperbacks; sell via Amazon storefront; optional ISBN provided.
  • Operational fit: high—once formatting is solved, it’s largely maintenance-light.
DigitalPrint-on-demandHigh scale

itch.io (Digital goods)

Best for: PDFs (comics), devlogs, soundtracks, art packs, early builds, bundles.

  • Cost: free to create pages and upload content.
  • Free capabilities: pay-what-you-want; configurable revenue share; direct or collected payouts.
  • Operational fit: very high—simple storefront + community hub.
DigitalCommunityFlexible pricing

Gumroad (Direct digital storefront)

Best for: PDFs, asset packs, zines, artbooks, toolkits; direct-to-fan sales.

  • Cost: no required subscription; fees apply per sale.
  • Free capabilities: pay-what-you-want via “0+” pricing; discount codes; file delivery.
  • Operational fit: high—single-person manageable.
DigitalDirect-to-fanSolo-friendly

Ko-fi (Shop + donations)

Best for: lightweight shop, tips, memberships, and digital downloads alongside community posts.

  • Cost: free to start; service fees on shop/membership features depending on mode.
  • Free capabilities: shop listings; digital/physical items; significant storage and item limits.
  • Operational fit: high—good “support hub” + small storefront.
DigitalSupport hubLow overhead

2) Print-on-Demand & Dropshipping (Physical goods)

Lulu (Print-on-demand books)

Best for: higher-quality books (artbooks, comics collections, lore volumes) + optional distribution.

  • Cost: free to create an account and publish; pay per print job + shipping.
  • Free capabilities: PoD fulfillment; guides/templates; no order minimums.
  • Operational fit: medium-high—slightly more setup than KDP, but stable once configured.
Print-on-demandBooksNo minimums

Printify (Dropshipping merch)

Best for: posters, apparel, mugs, simple merch. You upload designs; they fulfill orders.

  • Cost: free to use; optional paid plan exists but not required.
  • Free capabilities: create products; connect to up to a limited number of stores; pay per order/fulfillment.
  • Operational fit: medium—more SKUs, returns, and customer support considerations.
DropshippingMerchPay-per-order

3) Comics-Specific Distribution

GlobalComix

Best for: serialized comics + digital discovery in a comic-native audience.

  • Cost: free creator tier available.
  • Free capabilities: unlimited uploads; analytics; scheduled publishing; team roles; monetization tools.
  • Operational fit: medium-high—works well if you can publish consistently (even in “seasons”).
