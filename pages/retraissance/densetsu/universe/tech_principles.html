Tech Principles

Technological doctrine in Densetsu: engineered systems, predictive frameworks, and their interaction with magic and time. Pair with Magic Principles for a dual view.

Foundations

  • Predictive systems (e.g., Kiputer) can clash with World Memory/Chronobonsai if they overwrite state.
  • Hybrid tech-magic artifacts (Chronobonsai, Concord devices) must respect anomaly thresholds.
