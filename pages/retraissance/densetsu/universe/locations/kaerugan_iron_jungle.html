Kaerugan: The Iron Jungle

Overview

Techno-mystic industrial realm where nature and machine merge; trees hum with memory; entropy forges reclaim BE metal; home to Rustborn cults and guild oligarchs. “Kaerugan” and “the Iron Jungle” refer to the same belt of reclaimed arcologies within Verdathal.

Narrative role

Frontier workshop of the Western Continent: players broker between salvagers, eco-guardians, and smugglers, deciding how far industrial expansion can go before it poisons allied realms. Doubles as a lore node tying Verdathal industry to the Ashen Sands power bleed.

Known facts

  • Once vassal under King Frog; now the Kaerugan Industrial Federation pushes independence.
  • Rustfall (metal rain) and Rust Bloom events mark entropy engine activity; Rustmoss thrives near foundries.
  • Hitomi left living code for Michio here; rumors of a fuel-less fire wielder.
  • Ashen dust drifts east, darkening Eresh skies and trade routes.

Mechanics impact

Environmental hazards (toxic smog, metal rain), faction reputation checks between industrialists and eco-guardians, salvage/entropy crafting loops, and possible chrono-tech experiments that link to time anomalies.

Relationships

  • Verdathal Commonwealth: Parent polity; exports industrial output and reclaimed scrap.
  • Eresh Emirate / Ashen Sands: Receives ash drifts and power bleed from Kaerugan networks.
  • Galport Trade Federation: Brokers smuggled parts and exotic scrap.
  • Technogarch Civilization: Buried tech remnants feed the Iron Jungle’s reclamation.
