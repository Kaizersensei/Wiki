Eresh Emirate

Overview

Desert emirate built atop buried pre-echo / Technogarch infrastructure. Mesopotamian-inspired culture, excavation-driven economy, and ash-choked skies from distant industrial mills shape daily life. Lies over the imploded Mirror Spire core and entombed Technogarch arcologies (now Ashen Sands).

Narrative role

Acts as the Western desert hub for relic hunting and dream politics. A place where players weigh ancient tech reuse against cultural preservation, and where “dream empires” trade influence through caravans and covenants.

Known facts

  • Excavation-first economy that repurposes buried Technogarch/Old World tech.
  • Ashy sky comes from volcanic mills beyond its borders (imported pollution) plus residual heat vents near the Spire.
  • Mesopotamian-inspired architecture and governance.
  • Controls caravan routes that link Galport, Kaerugan, and Verdathal markets.
  • Sits above the buried Mirror Spire core; residual heat/anomalies shape survival and trade. Kiputer recursion still echoes through the buried arrays.

Buried infrastructure (Metal Bones)

Built atop Builders of Eternity megastructures—the "Metal Bones of the World"—and the buried Technogarch arcologies that powered the Mirror Spire. Broken metal structures, abandoned machines, and the imploded Spire core still bleed heat and bioelectric bursts. Excavation fuels the Emirate's economy but risks reactivating unstable chrono-tech and resurfacing Eclipse causality.

Mechanics impact

Likely hosts relic appraisal, caravan reputation tracks, excavation dungeons, and survival gating (heat/overheat). Expect tread-machine traversal, excavation gear checks, ancient-tech traps, and anomaly interactions near the Mirror Spire. Dream-state interactions could gate side quests or fast-travel anchors.

Relationships

  • Galport: Maritime partner for relic export; shares intel on sea rifts.
  • Kaerugan: Supplies raw scrap/tech; depends on Kaerugan’s reassembly expertise.
  • Verdathal: Trades for balanced tech-nature solutions; contrasts Verdathal’s restraint.
  • Eddara: Tense over ash pollution; exchanges reagents for purification rites.
