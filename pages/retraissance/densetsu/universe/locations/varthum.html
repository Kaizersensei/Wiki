Varthum

Overview

Mountain plateaus of temporal scholars where citizens are snared in repeating loops. Varthum guards deep time-knowledge and dangerous anomalies.

Narrative role

Late-arc temporal hub (around Arc 8). A place to learn how echoes, loops, and world memories work. Hitomi was imprisoned here in a temporal loop while seeking memory crystals; her flame projections reach out only through memories that contain fire. Mirror Bastion is confirmed to be in Varthum, not Seigetsu.

Known facts

  • Citizens and scholars are caught in recursive time loops.
  • Holds a repository of temporal knowledge; anomaly “Chrono-Imprint” is present.
  • Mirror Bastion stands here as a fortified temporal site.
  • Mountain plateau geography; isolation aids containment.

Mechanics impact

Expect loop-based quests, memory crystal collection, and anomaly mitigation (Chrono-Imprint). Possible time-locked doors, resettable encounters, and flame-projection communications (Hitomi) as a mechanic gate.

Relationships

  • Seigetsu Empire: Watches Varthum's temporal research; past conflicts seeded oversight tension.
  • Ninth Wave: Ambushed Hitomi here; may seek to exploit time tech.
  • Western realms: Seek guidance on handling rifts/loops; trade knowledge for protection.
