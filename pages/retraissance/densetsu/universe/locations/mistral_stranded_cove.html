Mistral Stranded Cove

Overview

Time-fractured hidden cove where the ship “Mistral” is stranded; tides and currents from different eras collide, leaving the sea path broken.

Encounter

  • Captain Levantera (traveler from a shattered nautical age) hums by the fire; coat always caught in wind.
  • Unlockable via relic quest; echoes of sailors heard as distant shanties.

Notes

Illustrates time-confused oceans; offers wind/sea motif bonuses (sea breeze dodge, slight wind affinity).
