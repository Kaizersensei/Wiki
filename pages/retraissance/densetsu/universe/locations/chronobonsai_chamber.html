Chronobonsai Chamber

Overview

Hidden chamber inside the Bonsai Sage’s ancient tree; a relic machine “crafted after history” to walk through time, locked to Michio’s bloodline.

Story beats

  • Revealed during the Hirogawa attack; Sage entrusts Michio to find his parents and fix the broken weave of time.
  • Interior blends bark, biomechanical tendrils, and future machinery; hums like a heartbeat.

Notes

Accessed via the Chronobonsai/Infinitree; serves as primary time-travel mechanism for the narrative.
