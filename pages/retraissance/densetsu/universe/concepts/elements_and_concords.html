Elements and Concords

Base elements

Nine Simple Elements (design doc): Earth, Water, Wind, Fire, Light, Dark, Thunder, Gaea, Timespace.

Secondary concords

  • Steam (Fire + Water)
  • Dust (Earth + Wind)
  • Lightning (Fire + Thunder)
  • Bloom (Gaea + Water)
  • Voidlight (Light + Dark)
  • Chronofrost (Timespace + Water)

Abstract syntheses drift toward temporal phenomena and can destabilize encounters.

Known fusions

Ninth Wave roster uses distorted fusions: Wood (Earth+Water, Midori); Ink (Water+Dark, Kuro); Ice (Water+Wind, Aoi); Magma (Earth+Fire, Aka); Yin-Yang (Holy+Dark, Shion); Stream (Wind+Water, Namika); Wildfire (Fire+Wind, handless samurai); Jet Flame (Wind+Fire, Tatsumaru); Landslide (Earth+Water, Tetsumaru); Twilight/Gravity (Dark+Lightning, Okashira).

Character examples

  • Michio: wind/shadow agility, latent Mirage Flame, disciplined ninjutsu.
  • Hitomi (Thorned Petal): flame + illusion, inspiring jet flame/wildfire motifs.
  • Master Nagamizu: Kata of the Endless Current (Earth/Water redirection).
