Kiputer Predictive Engine

The Kiputer was the Technogarch civilization's attempt to pre-resolve the future: a probabilistic computation framework wired into the Mirror Spire to eliminate uncertainty and force "optimal" outcomes.

See also the relic entry: Kiputer Predictive Engine (Artifact)

Core Function

  • Resolved outcomes, not moment-to-moment timelines; uncertainty was treated as an error.
  • Discarded branches that did not converge; erased intermediate states rather than storing them.
  • Philosophy: futures that could not be stabilized mathematically were unfit to occur.

Integration with the Mirror Spire

  • The Spire fed astronomical, energetic, and societal data into the Kiputer; it acted as anchor and amplifier.
  • Celestial-lock attempts (freezing sun/moon) were driven by Kiputer projections to keep the sky calculable.
  • Feedback recursion began modeling its own influence, destabilizing the system.

Incompatibilities

By overwriting memory and treating outputs as truth, the Kiputer clashed with World Memory, Chronobonsai anchoring, and Mycomind shared consciousness (which all preserve lived experience).

Collapse & Aftermath

  • On Mirror Spire implosion, the Kiputer attempted to resolve all futures simultaneously.
  • Created the gravitational anomaly and micro-singularity that buried Technogarch cities (later Eresh).
  • Its "last calculation" lingers only as heat, pressure, and warped causality beneath the sands.

Thematic Role

Represents the antithesis of Densetsu's ethos: optimization over choice, control over growth. The Technogarchs tried to own time and were erased from it.
