Anomalies

Overview

Anomalies are localized breakdowns of temporal and elemental stability. They manifest as spaces where causality frays, physics distorts, or memory and matter refuse to agree on a single outcome.

Narrative role

They signal places of unresolved consequence: remnants of the Great Eclipse, failed corrections, or willful resistance to fate. Anomalies drive quests, gate travel, and surface forgotten lore.

Known facts

  • Signatures include distorted physics/causality, repeating events or frozen moments, and altered creature behavior.
  • They are symptoms of deeper imbalance; often adjacent to time rifts but not identical.
  • Resolution comes from stabilization, understanding, or ritual—not brute destruction.

Mechanics impact

  • Encounters: anomalous zones can change combat rules, AI behavior, or elemental interactions.
  • Exploration: loops or freezes local time; requires observation and pattern-breaking to exit.
  • Systems hooks: ties to temporal/elemental meters; resolving an anomaly can restore safe travel or unlock routes.

Relationships

  • Time Rifts are the visible ruptures; anomalies are the systemic consequences around them.
  • Factions may exploit or quarantine anomalies (e.g., Crimson Dynasty, Mycomind Covenant).
  • World state: anomaly density often rises near major events (see Great Eclipse and Calendar BE/AE).
