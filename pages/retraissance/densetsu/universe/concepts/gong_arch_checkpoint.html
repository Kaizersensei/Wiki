Gong Arch (Checkpoint)

Overview

Checkpoint that binds progress through ritual resonance. Striking the arch-gong syncs the player to the Infinitree cadence, setting a temporal anchor.

Narrative role

Embodies disciplined, ceremonial safeguarding of time-flow in Eastern-inspired regions. Reinforces that stability comes from resonance, not domination.

Known facts

  • Diegetic checkpoint linked to Infinitree roots via sound/vibration.
  • Can dispel minor local anomalies when activated.
  • Placed at temple thresholds, cliff shrines, or canyon passes.

Mechanics impact

Save/respawn point; may reset minor debuffs. Possible resonance buffs (parry/stamina efficiency) while active. Align with Engine checkpoint handling.

Relationships

  • Infinitree: resonance-based anchor.
  • Orb Statue: parallel checkpoint with different ritual.
  • Chronobonsai: natural shard variant.
