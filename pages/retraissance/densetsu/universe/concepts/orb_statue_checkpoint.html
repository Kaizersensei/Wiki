Orb Statue (Checkpoint)

Overview

Checkpoint based on holding an orb before a statue to harmonize with the Infinitree. Light pulses set the temporal anchor.

Narrative role

Represents devotional stability—quiet, reverent anchoring versus martial resonance. Often appears in sanctuaries or ruins where guardianship is passive.

Known facts

  • Diegetic checkpoint tied to Infinitree roots via light/energy instead of sound.
  • Activation may cleanse minor corruption nearby.
  • Placed in shrines, caves, and sites of forgotten guardians.

Mechanics impact

Save/respawn point; may restore minor status effects. Potential light-based buffs (clarity/awareness) while active. Align with Engine checkpoint handling.

Relationships

  • Infinitree: harmonized anchor.
  • Gong Arch: resonance counterpart.
  • Chronobonsai: natural shard variant.
