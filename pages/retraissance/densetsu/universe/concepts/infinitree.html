Infinitree

Overview

The Infinitree is a trans-temporal organism that anchored reality at the Great Eclipse. All Chronobonsai trees merged into it at Year 0, reigniting the stalled sun–moon cycle and ending the Eclipse’s physical lock. Its roots thread through time layers, stabilizing rifts and anomalies. It cannot be controlled or replicated; it represents endurance rather than dominance over time.

Narrative role

Acts as the impartial witness and stabilizer of the setting: the Bonsai Sage arrives alongside its rooting; Michio’s journey is guided by Infinitree-linked shrines and checkpoints. The Timeline Log it keeps is the closest thing to an unbiased history.

Known facts

  • Rooted across fractured timelines at the Eclipse, creating a stable anchor; formed by sacrificing all Chronobonsai into one tree.
  • Maintains an objective Timeline Log of events (contrasts with subjective World Memories).
  • Stabilizes anomalies and temporal rifts but does not erase them.
  • Cannot be replicated or "tamed"; attempts to dominate time lead to corruption.
  • Bonsai Sage is its primary caretaker/interpreter; Chronobonsai shrines echo its function and precede its formation.

Mechanics impact

Guides anomaly resolution rather than time rewind. Checkpoints (e.g., Chronobonsai) are diegetic links to its roots. Access to the Timeline Log gates verification of canon events and powers truth-aligned features.

Relationships

  • World Memory vs Timeline Log: Infinitree stores the objective side.
  • Bonsai Sage: caretaker and interpreter of the Infinitree.
  • Chronobonsai: shard-like checkpoints tied to its roots.
  • Great Eclipse: moment of rooting and setting-wide stabilization.
