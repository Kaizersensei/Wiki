Silkmother

Summary

Brood matriarch that anchors webs across anomaly seams, tethering victims to repeating moments. Hunts in forest/marsh anomalies, cocooning prey and using silk to hold space in place.

Behavior & phases

  • Web anchors that pin victims to looped moments; cocoons as tethers.
  • Summons spiderling swarms; enrages with silk-lash when pressured.

Weakness

  • Fire severs silk anchors; destroying pylons/cocoons interrupts regeneration.
