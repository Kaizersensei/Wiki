Raijin's Last Apprentice

Summary

Thunder duelist channeling Raijin forms; swaps between spear and barehand lightning seals. Punishes aggression with parry-shocks.

Behavior & phases

  • Quick dashes, charged thrusts, and area lightning calls.
  • Stance swaps between spear form and barehand sealing strikes.
  • Parry-shock retaliation on careless aggression.

Weakness

  • Grounding with water/mud fields reduces lightning damage and opens punish windows.
