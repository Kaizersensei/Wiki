The Lingering Oath

Summary

Oathbound spirit knight guarding a forgotten vow; replays echoes of recent combat to punish repetition. Duelist encounter tied to a vow circle of sigils.

Behavior & mechanics

  • Echo memory: Mirrors the last three player moves; repetition is punished.
  • Vow circle: Ground sigils anchor the vow; breaking them forces a vulnerable state.

Weakness

  • Destroy/disrupt the vow sigils to open it up.
  • Vary move patterns to avoid echo counters.
