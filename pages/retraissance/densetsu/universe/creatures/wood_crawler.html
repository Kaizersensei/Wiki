Behavior & Notes

  • Camouflages as fallen or standing timber, remaining motionless until prey nears; first strike is a crushing pounce followed by constricting coils.
  • Wooden faceplate hides sensory pits; bludgeoning or fire can crack it, reducing accuracy and aggression.
  • Territorial and self-sustaining—may remain in an area for years, slowly overgrowing paths unless culled.
  • Legendary elder variant exists: tougher bark, can overgrow entire clearings, described as “inverse of Kodomaru.”
