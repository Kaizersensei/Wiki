Crimson Courts

Alignment

Lawful Authoritarian / Utilitarian Control — stability and perception of order outrank truth, justice, or individual life.

Structure

  • Multi-member masked judiciary; masks represent roles (Dragon, Ox, Snake, etc.), not persons.
  • Judges act independently but escalate collectively; fragmentation avoids accountability.
  • Authority is role-bound, not personality-bound.

Methods of control

  • Public ritual in the Arena of San Jiang to legitimize rulings.
  • Quiet inspections for compliance between spectacles.
  • Fire as controlled displacement; accusations as regulatory tools, not moral judgments.

Known figures

  • Masked Judges: Anonymous, role-bound authorities enforcing doctrine.
  • Arena Champion (Yǒng Lóng Quán): Instrument of spectacle and containment.
  • Court Soldiers: Disciplined enforcers trained for procedure over initiative.

Doctrine vs. reality

Public doctrine: Boiling salt water is alchemy.

Practical reality: Courts already know the river is compromised; potable water is stockpiled and rationed silently.

Relationship to the Ninth Wave

  • Heavy “immunity taxes” paid to avoid direct interference.
  • Resource shortages partially caused by Ninth Wave coercion—an intentionally unspoken arrangement.
  • Revealed later in connection with revolutionary pressures.

Related links

  • Crimson Dynasty — state structure the courts serve and insulate.
  • Crimson Culture — language and behaviors that normalize court authority.
  • Ninth Wave — external pressure shaping court concessions.
