Hirogawa Feud

Canon Overview

First major stronghold in the story, founded and led by Lord Ishigane as a buffer between empires. Trains defenders (Hanzo, Hitomi, Michio, Nagamizu lineage) and resists incursions like the Hirogawa Town Fire/Ninth Wave assault.

Snapshot

  • Era: Current
  • Region: Eastern
  • Rule type: Daimyo / feudal ward
  • Leader: Lord Ishigane
  • Ways: Samurai discipline, local militia drills, fan/blade schools (Nagamizu lineage), community defense
  • Goals: Protect villagers, hold the line against invasions (Ninth Wave/Seigetsu remnants), shelter survivors
  • Trade routes: Limited local exchange with neighboring Aokuni villages; occasional caravans to Galport via inland routes

Operations & Ways

  • Training courtyard culture (Nagamizu); combined arms from Ishigane’s command.
  • Defensive posture; mobilizes quickly against raids.
