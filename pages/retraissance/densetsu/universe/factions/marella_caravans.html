Marella Caravans (Blackwater Tidebrokers)

Canon Overview

Shadow caravan network of moray-like traders led by Manolo Marella. Operates underwater Blackwater trench routes with bioluminescent waypoints and Ki resonance beacons to move relics and pre-Eclipse tech outside official channels.

Snapshot

  • Era: Current
  • Region: Western
  • Rule type: Shadow economy guild
  • Leader: Manolo Marella (“Tidebroker”)
  • Ways: Underwater trench corridors; Ki beacon navigation; favors/rare currency
  • Goals: Control illicit tech/relic flow; outmaneuver Galport blockades; profit through secrecy
  • Trade routes: Verdathal tech lanes; Blackwater ports; covert exchanges with Eddara/Timberborn

Operations & Economy

  • Smuggling corridors: hidden trenches, beacon-guided lanes, favors/rare currency economy.
  • Markets: optional vendors/quest givers; affects market stability and crafting resources.
  • Quest hook “The Depth Between Deals”: aid smuggling Verdathal tech past Galport blockade or expose them.

Leadership & Assets

  • Leader: Manolo Marella (“Tidebroker,” monocled).
  • Assets: trench maps, bioluminescent waypoints, Ki beacons, Blackwater crews.
