Fire Dominion

Canon Overview

Historic empire of solar engineers and philosopher-kings. Forged molten-metal architecture from the Ember Citadel until rebellion consumed the last Fire Emperor. Rituals and aesthetics echo through the Crimson Dynasty.

Snapshot

  • Era: Historic (pre-Eclipse dynasties)
  • Region: Eastern
  • Rule type: Imperial monarchy
  • Leader: The Fire Emperor (name lost); philosopher-kings
  • Ways: Solar engineering, molten forging, ritualized metallurgy, radiant city-building
  • Goals: Expand solar dominion, immortalize rule through enduring metalworks
  • Trade routes: Overland convoys for ore and fuel; imperial tribute networks (largely lost)

Operations & Ways

  • Structured society around solar forges and philosopher courts.
  • Weapon and architecture design used molten metal; Ember Citadel was the capital furnace-city.
