Eddara Council of Elders

Canon Overview

Governing council of Eddara, a nature realm on the Western continent. Elders (including the Dog Elder) steward oral traditions, relic shards, and shard retrieval guidance.

Snapshot

  • Era: Current
  • Region: Western
  • Rule type: Council of elders
  • Leader: Collective council; Dog Elder as a key member
  • Ways: Oral law, relic stewardship, nature rites
  • Goals: Protect Eddara shards/biomes, guide travelers (e.g., relic quests), maintain autonomy
  • Trade routes: Limited barter with Galport/Verdathal; favors over currency
