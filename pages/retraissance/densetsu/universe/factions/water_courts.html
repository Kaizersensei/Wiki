Water Courts

Canon Overview

Historic island and river kingdoms bound by tide and ritual. Innovated resonance-stone communications and mapped temporal flows in ocean currents. Many maritime customs persist in Galport’s navigators.

Snapshot

  • Era: Historic (pre-Eclipse dynasties)
  • Region: Islands and river realms
  • Rule type: Maritime courts / ritual monarchy
  • Leader: Tide-bound regents (names lost)
  • Ways: Tide rites, resonance-stone messaging, temporal current charting
  • Goals: Steward waterways, synchronize tides/ritual calendars, secure maritime law
  • Trade routes: Inter-island convoys; precursor lanes to later Galport routes

Operations & Ways

  • Communicated across courts via resonance stones tuned to tidal cycles.
  • First to model timeflow in currents, enabling safer crossings and temporal forecasts.
