Torredasso

Overview

Giant Manueline-inspired mech relic: Belém-tower silhouette, Portuguese metalwork filigree, armillary-sphere joints, and a funicular winch system for vertical mobility. Can be restored and recruited if a repair side-quest is completed.

Known facts

  • Built as an armored construct; treated in lore as a relic-class machine rather than a living enemy.
  • Armillary joints imply rotational locks and exposed bearings—potential weak points or maintenance ports.
  • Winch-driven vertical movement suggests arena/mountain usage where elevation control matters.
  • Post-repair: may return to aid the player as part of Don Bastion's reformed army after Saul Azar's supporters are tried and sentenced.

Open questions / conflicts

  • Origin and allegiance unknown (Kaerugan industrial lineage? Eresh salvage repurposed?).
  • Need canonical AI/phase description if used as a boss fight or setpiece.
  • Define loot/quest hooks only when established elsewhere.
  • Specify companion behaviors when allied (support role, traversal utility, or combat assist?).
