Marella Sea Horn

Overview

Abyssal shell horn tuned to tidal pulse; crafted by Marella Caravans. Blown at coastal/underwater resonance points to summon a temporary Blackwater vendor.

Usage

  • Works only at marked resonance cues (bioluminescent coral / sound hints).
  • Signals through Mycelium Neural Network and Blackwater Channels.
  • Overuse outside valid zones can attract Galport patrols or hostile sealife.

Rewards

Vendors offer contraband, deep-sea relics, and undersea crafting materials (Abyssal Pearl, Luminescent Scalecloth, Forgotten Relic Core).
