Kiputer Predictive Engine

Canon Details

Technogarch relic that tried to “pre-resolve” futures by eliminating uncertainty. Integrated into the Mirror Spire; attempted to lock celestial cycles, driving the forced Great Eclipse convergence. Stored no memories—overwrote them—making it hostile to World Memory, Chronobonsai, and Mycomind systems.

Nature

  • Conceptual computation lattice, not a single chassis; treated outcomes as truth and discarded intermediate states.
  • Modeled probability collapse, rejected futures it could not stabilize.
  • Feedback recursion modeled its own influence, destabilizing the Spire.

Mirror Spire Link

  • Spire fed astronomical, energetic, and societal data into the Kiputer to freeze orbits and sky cycles.
  • Great Eclipse framed as intentional convergence; Kiputer could not account for recursion and self-interference.
  • Final state attempted to resolve all futures, leaving heat/pressure/gravity scars beneath Eresh.

Remaining Traces

  • Fragments in hieroglyphic depictions: “thinking suns,” “silent clocks,” “blind certainty.”
  • Environmental anomalies around the Mirror Spire and Ashen Sands.
  • Technogarch-era scrap and possible data shards buried under Eresh.

Open Questions / Conflicts

  • Exact hardware form factor(s); whether any Kiputer node survived intact.
  • Whether Technogarchs seeded remote nodes beyond the Spire.
  • Interaction rules if recovered: contamination risks to World Memory/Chronobonsai anchors.
