Midori

Overview

Midori — The Blooming Shade is the Green Current general of the Ninth Wave (Earth + Water -> Wood). She wins by shaping terrain, misdirecting attention, and turning “order” into quiet occupation.

Identity

  • Role: One of Okashira’s Eight Currents; Wood-element general (Blooming Shade).
  • Faction: Ninth Wave.
  • Elements: Wood (fusion of Earth + Water); illusionist/battlefield control.
  • Story position: Early-arc antagonist proving Ninth Wave leaders are human, motivated, and dangerous.

Appearance

  • Silhouette: Light, mobile, deliberately unarmored—“dancer in a war zone.”
  • Markers: Short dark-blue hair, green eyes, fox-fur mantle over a long cape (emerald lining); practical outfit for rapid pivots and vertical movement.
  • Palette: Greens/browns—reads as forest dweller rather than uniformed officer.

Personality

  • Composed, curious, strategic—tests how people behave under pressure and misdirection.
  • Cruelty as procedure: harm delivered as “process,” not rage.
  • Youth as blade and shield: confident exterior with taught overconfidence beneath.

Abilities & Tactics

  • Living misdirection: controls space, footing, and attention via Wood affinity.
  • Signature techniques: Log Substitution (short teleports with clones), Ambush Teleport (behind targets or interactables), Star Shard Barrage (fan of sharpened wood), Love Spell AOE (brief stun), Wood Golem ultimate (arena shift).
  • Style: Punishes aggression, rewards patience; anchors casts to bamboo/log points and environmental cover.

Role in Hirogawa (First Arc)

  • Runs the Bamboo Thicket operation: enslaved workers mass-produce Hagane Bamboo canes for fortification.
  • Introduces the hostage pattern: keeps a private captive, turning the fight into a moral constraint.
  • Sets tone: Ninth Wave are sentient and calculating; “truth can be present and still be obscured.”

Themes & Signals

  • Human antagonists: interiority, doubts, pride—more than rank insignia.
  • Order as control: labor ritualized; efficiency masking occupation.
  • War as procedure: cruelty executed as routine rather than spectacle.

Hooks / Open Questions

  • How she chose the Ninth Wave (debt, oath, ideology?).
  • Origin and what was lost before her rank.
  • Why she keeps a personal hostage (leverage, mirror, punishment?).
