Visual traits

Ice-scarred warlord; thin goatee; frost motifs; bearing of the Ninth Wave’s commander.

Identity

  • Origin: Yukigami, orphan of the Hyoken clan.
  • Role: Leader of the Ninth Wave; time-fracturing warlord.
  • Elements: Ice-wielder; aligned with temporal manipulation through force.

Ideology vs. Kiputer

  • Certainty over choice: Sees suffering as a mismanaged system; believes the world fails because it is allowed to choose.
  • Kiputer fixation: Treats the Technogarch predictive engine as a “silent god” that erases mercy and ambiguity. Wants to finish what the Technogarchs started.
  • Fatal flaw: Thinks the Kiputer failed in execution, not premise; ignores that it erases memory and collapses when faced with compassionate defiance.

Philosophical triangle

  • Bonsai Sage: Observes, predicts, intervenes minimally.
  • Chronobonsai / Infinitree: Stabilize time via lived memory.
  • Kiputer / Okashira: Delete memory in favor of outcome certainty; seeks results without accountability.

Narrative payoff

Positions Okashira as the last inheritor of Technogarch hubris—trying to remove responsibility from the present as they removed choice from the future. His attempt to harness the Kiputer would yield unstable, self-collapsing projections.
