Kodomaru

Summary

Living wooden guardian whose body tolerates the Mirror Spire’s reactor heat/temporal distortion. Essential to the Eresh arc; serves as both mechanical key and mythic anchor.

Role in Eresh / reactor access

  • Only viable interface near the ruined reactor core; wood composition resists heat/radiation-like energy and temporal distortion (tolerance, not immunity).
  • Resonates with Technogarch systems: dormant mechanisms briefly reactivate, sealed paths unlock, stabilization sequences become possible.
  • Pip must wear Kodomaru as living armor (Eresh expedition): unfolding exoskeleton, heavy/tank-like momentum, frog-like leaps, ground slams. Hazards become survivable, not harmless.
  • Not a vehicle—he is a burden willingly carried.

Mythic implications

  • Ancient hieroglyph-like records show a “tree-shaped being” shielding a ruler from unbearable light/heat; predates Technogarch collapse.
  • Suggests Technogarchs borrowed/replicated an older solution; mystery preserved (strange man could be Bonsai Sage/predecessor/echo).
  • Environmental cues: heat bends away, hostile machinery falls silent near him, reactor chambers echo the hieroglyph motifs.

Themes

  • Survival through coexistence, not domination.
  • Protection through life (wood) instead of steel.
  • The cost of carrying what others abandoned.
