Timeline Index

Overview

The timeline is a curated index of consequential events, not a full chronology. Entries prioritize impact over exhaustiveness.

Narrative role

Anchors arcs, foreshadows echoes, and preserves contradictions where sources disagree. Absence of record does not imply absence of occurrence.

Known facts

  • Organized by arcs, not by year counts; AE dating is used where possible.
  • Parallel/conflicting entries are allowed when sources diverge.
  • Many entries branch from Great Eclipse echoes, rifts, or localized anomalies.

Mechanics impact

  • Index should surface arcs, locations, and participants rather than strict dates.
  • Entries can expose conditions (quest locks/unlocks, stability states) instead of fixed times.
  • Pairs with autolink to connect factions, locations, characters, and events pages.

Relationships

  • Rooted in Great Eclipse echoes and BE/AE calendar shifts.
  • Feeds into faction relationships as conflicts realign.
  • Should cross-link to specific event pages (e.g., Hirogawa Town Fire) as they are authored.
