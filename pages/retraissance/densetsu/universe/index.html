Bimmy's Bio (According to Local Myth)

Calendar

The Great Eclipse split history into BE (Before Eclipse) and AE (After Eclipse). The current age is 1326 AE. BE/AE are not perfectly convertible; the Eclipse distorted chronological flow.

World Shape

Two vast continents (Eastern: Aokuni-rooted, spiritual harmony; Western: diverse, industrial ambition/forgotten gods) plus island nations (e.g., Mycomind Covenant, Kojimigakure). Intercontinental crossings are rare and controlled; the Eclipse fractured geography and stars.

Progression

Regional arc order (draft): Arc1 Hirogawa; Arc2 Crimson Dynasty; Arc3 Seigetsu; Arc4 Yukigami; Arc5 Eddara; Arc6 Galport (Boalis); Arc7 Verdathal; Arc8 Varthum; Arc9 Eresh Emirate; post-Galport free-roam with Mycomind/Kojimigakure access.

Open questions / conflicts

Open: hierarchy between time rifts and anomalies not locked; naming conventions (full names vs given names) not fully locked. Time travel remains non-player-controlled; chat canon overrides docs.
