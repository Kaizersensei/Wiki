Eastern Continent

Summary

The Eastern Continent is shaped by long-standing traditions and elemental philosophy. Cultures emphasize balance, discipline, and ritual even as temporal scars remain.

Characteristics

  • Strong cultural continuity despite temporal damage.
  • Emphasis on balance, discipline, and ritual across lineages.
  • Time damage is respected, not challenged; instability is navigated carefully.

Narrative role

Anchors continuity and restraint; contrasts regions that exploit anomalies or salvage tech aggressively.

Mechanics impact

  • Favor systems that reward patience and ritual completion.
  • Traversal is comparatively stable but bound by etiquette around anomalies.

Relationships

  • Acts as a cultural counterweight to industrial/desert scavenger regions.
  • Negotiates crossings with respect for temporal boundaries.
