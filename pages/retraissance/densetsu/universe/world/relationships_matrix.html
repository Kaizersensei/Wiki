Faction Relationships

Summary

Relationships between factions shift with temporal phases, grievance cycles, and access to anomalies/resources. Alliances are provisional and context-specific.

Destabilizing factors

  • Temporal phase: stability swings with rifts/echoes; routes open/close unpredictably.
  • Historical grievances: contradictory records cause mismatched expectations between partners.
  • Resource/anomaly access: control of crossings, salvage sites, or temporal tech realigns blocs.

Illustrative ties

  • Galport – Eddara: treaty arc "Wings of Accord" to lift flight curse; see Galport and Harbormaster Compact.
  • Eresh – Kaerugan – Crimson: dream-binding vs entropy vs elemental courts; see Eresh Emirate and Kaerugan Federation.
  • Varthum – Eresh/Galport: memory tech exchange (Salt Libraries, Gatekeeper quests); see Varthum.
  • Verdathal – Eddara: bio/forest tech cooperation; see Verdathal.
  • Ninth Wave vs all: antagonist pursuing Infinitree/Chronobonsai; destabilizes crossings.
  • Neutral/Outside: Mycomind and Kojimigakure stay lateral to mainland blocs.

Notes

  • All alliances provisional; adjust per local temporal stability.
