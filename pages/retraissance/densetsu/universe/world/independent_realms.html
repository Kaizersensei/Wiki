Independent Realms

Overview

Independent realms sit outside major continental power structures and often ignore AE historical norms. They preserve local truths that mainland powers have lost or overwritten.

Traits

  • Geographically isolated or otherwise hard to access.
  • Governed by unique belief systems or biological collectives.
  • Limited adherence to AE dating; may preserve truths lost elsewhere.

Notes

  • Expect mismatched timelines and atypical diplomacy; crossings may require bespoke rites.
  • Catalog candidate realms as pages are added; link when location/faction entries exist.
