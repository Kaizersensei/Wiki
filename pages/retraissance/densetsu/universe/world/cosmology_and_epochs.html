Cosmology & Epochs

Overview

The world exists across layered temporal strata rather than a single linear history. The Great Eclipse is the only absolute cosmological constant.

Epochs

  • BE (Before Eclipse): mythic, unstable, partially overwritten.
  • AE (After Eclipse): stabilized but fractured reality.

Cosmological model

  • Time behaves as an ecosystem; space retains memory.
  • Some locations exist in overlapping states.
  • Balance favors correction, not prevention.

Timeline anchors

  • ~674 BE: Chronobonsai roots (Infinitree).
  • 0 AE: Great Eclipse (elements halted; calendar split).
  • 1313 AE: Hanzo disappears.
  • 1326 AE: Current age; Hirogawa attack; Galport treaty arc.

Belief frames (selected)

  • Dreaming Faith (Eresh): time as World-Dream; vision-led rule (see Eresh Emirate).
  • Chronosophs: time as measurable structure; friction with dream-centric faiths.
  • Recurring motifs: Bonsai Tree, Leaf Sigil, Reverse Moon (links to Time Fan, Chronobonsai).
