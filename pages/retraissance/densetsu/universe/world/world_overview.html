World Overview

Overview

The world of Densetsu is a fractured, living system shaped by the Great Eclipse. Regions share consequence rather than origin: time bends unevenly, geography still bears elemental scars, and memory differs by place.

Narrative role

Anchors the setting’s tone: a world where history is layered, travel is perilous, and every region remembers the Eclipse differently. Provides the stage for temporal instability, factional struggle, and cultural divergence.

Known facts

  • Time behaves inconsistently across regions; timelines can loop, stall, or echo.
  • Geography reflects historical and elemental scars from the Eclipse.
  • Cultural memory varies; some regions deny the Eclipse, others live in its recurrences.
  • No region is truly isolated; temporal storms and faction controls gate reliable travel.

Mechanics impact

Encourages region-specific rules (travel costs, time anomalies, safe crossings), and supports temporal rift mechanics and phased world states (see Engine Runtime and Engine docs).

Western Continent

  • Galport
  • Eddara
  • Verdathal
  • Eresh
  • Varthum
Travel happens mostly over land except in some cases:

  • Galport <-> Eresh is done over sea, along the coast. A large mountain range makes crossing the border by land a very difficult task
  • Galport <-> Eddara is done mostly by air after Wings of Accord concludes.
  • Verdathal --> Whole continent can be traveled by river, but one-way only.
