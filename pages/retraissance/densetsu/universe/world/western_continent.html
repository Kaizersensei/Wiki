Western Continent

Overview

The Western Continent is culturally diverse and politically fragmented, defined by adaptation and reuse.

Narrative role

Represents improvisation and survival: factions repurpose technology and navigate frequent anomalies near excavation zones.

Known facts

  • Mix of industrial, maritime, and desert-influenced regions.
  • Heavy reliance on scavenged or repurposed technology.
  • Frequent temporal anomalies around excavation and salvage sites.

Mechanics impact

Supports mechanics around salvage, unstable traversal, and anomaly-prone zones; aligns with modular gear/systems (see Engine docs).

Relationships

  • Fragmented alliances; resource and anomaly access drive shifting pacts.
  • Often intersects with maritime trade routes and contested crossing points.
