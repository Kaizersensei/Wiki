Mycomind Covenant

Overview

The Mycomind Covenant is a fungal collective consciousness existing as an independent realm. It perceives time as continuity rather than rupture, making it an outlier in how the Eclipse is experienced.

Narrative role

Acts as a neutral archive and living witness. The Covenant can confirm or contradict other factions’ memories, and its timeless perspective can stabilize (or obscure) contested histories.

Known facts

  • Shared memory across hosts; individual bodies are temporary nodes.
  • Timeless perception of events; treats BE/AE as a continuous ledger.
  • Generally neutral toward external conflicts unless spores/threads are threatened.

Mechanics impact

  • Potential save/recall mechanic via spore-linked memory.
  • Quarantine or immunity rules in fungal zones; altered AI behavior for spore-bound fauna.
  • Stability zones that dampen anomalies or echo events when the Covenant cooperates.

Relationships

  • Independent realm status; trades knowledge with select factions (e.g., scholars from Galport).
  • Occasional tension with extractive powers (Crimson Dynasty, opportunistic guilds) seeking spore tech.
  • Linked to island routes and rare crossings; often mediates temporal inconsistencies near sea lanes.
