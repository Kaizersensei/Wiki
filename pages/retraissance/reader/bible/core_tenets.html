Bible View

Core Tenets

Dr. Sappo, lectures Eddara's youth on Time Anomalies. (Nano Banana concept)

  1. Time is layered: timeline logs, world memory, and personal recall rarely align.
  2. Power has a cost: every Concord, craft, or device binds itself to a debt.
  3. Legends converge: myths recur because the world rehearses what it fears and needs.
  4. Choice echoes: small acts resonate through Concords, lands, and even anomalies.
  5. Boundaries leak: realms touch; anomalies, covenants, and spires expose the seams.
  6. Silence is data: what is missing (burned, sealed, looped) is itself a signal.
  7. Nothing is alone: every entity is entangled—by root, oath, code, or circuit.
