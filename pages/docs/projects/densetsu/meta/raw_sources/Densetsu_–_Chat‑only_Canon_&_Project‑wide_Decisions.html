Densetsu – Chat‑Only Canon & Project‑Wide
Decisions
This document summarizes project‑wide changes, rules, and decisions that were defined or refined in
chats  only,  not  necessarily  reflected  in  formal  design  documents  at  the  time.  It  is  intended  as  a
consolidation reference for Codex, wiki generation, and future documentation updates.
1. Canon & Narrative Rules
1.1 Time, Causality, and Travel
Time travel is not a player‑controlled mechanic.
Time is a narrative element meant to be observed, not manipulated.
Attempts to manipulate time are thematically linked to madness, corruption, or world sickness.
Time rifts are not portals:
They are cracks in reality showing how past events unfolded beyond current knowledge.
They may also show possible future outcomes.
Their purpose is to provide subtle narrative or mechanical hints, not direct intervention.
Any future allowance for time interaction (e.g., New Game+) must be framed as alternate timelines,
not classical time travel.
1.2 Anomalies
Anomalies are not generic glitches.
They are consequences of temporal stress, refusal to accept outcomes, or unresolved causality.
Bosses and major conflicts may be manifestations of anomalies.
Relationship between Time Rifts and Anomalies exists but hierarchy is not yet locked (explicitly
flagged as an open decision).
2. World Structure & Geography
2.1 Continents (Chat‑Defined)
The world is split into two major continents plus independent regions:
Eastern Continent
Asian‑inspired cultures.
Includes Hirogawa, Crimson Dynasty, Seigetsu, Yukigami, Kojimigakure.
Western Continent
Culturally diverse, comparable to Europe.
Includes Kaerugan, Verdathal, Eddara, Galport.
Mycomind Covenant
Independent island nation.

2.2 Eresh Emirate (Chat Canon)
Mesopotamian‑inspired desert realm.
Governed by dream‑binding god‑kings.
Landscape filled with buried ancient machinery predating the Eclipse.
Exports scavenged metals and parts to Kaerugan.
Desert travel relies on cobbled‑together machines built from recovered tech.
3. Culture & Factions
3.1 Aokuni Culture
“Aokuni” is the canonical name for Japanese‑inspired cultures.
Applies to Eastern Continent regions unless overridden.
3.2 Marella Caravans / Blackwater Tidebrokers
Aquatic, non‑humanoid moray‑like beings.
Operate hidden underwater caravan routes.
Exist outside Galport law.
Gameplay role:
Optional vendors / quest givers.
Use favors or rare currencies instead of gold.
Narrative role:
Moral gray area.
Economic rivals yet necessary intermediaries.
Manolo Marella ("the Tidebroker") is visually defined as bourgeois (monocle explicitly noted).
4. Characters & Lineage (Clarifications from Chat)
Michio is the protagonist.
Hanzo is Michio’s father.
Hitomi is Michio’s mother.
Naming conventions (full names vs given names) are not yet fully locked and flagged as a gap.
5. Creatures vs Enemies (System‑Level Canon)
Creatures are treated internally as Actors but belong to a distinct Creature group.
Key distinctions:
Creatures act on instinct or environmental balance.
Enemies act with intent, allegiance, or corruption.
Creatures:
Can be hunted, studied, tamed, or ridden.

6. Gameplay Systems (Chat‑Defined or Refined)
6.1 Checkpoint System
Multiple checkpoint types are canon: - Chronobonsai - Transitional zones. - Act as checkpoint, silent loading
screen, auto‑save, and main menu. - Gong Arch - Area‑native structure holding a gong. - Activating it saves
progress without full reset. - Orb Statue - Refreshes HP and Ki. - Deity depiction depends on local culture.
Grouping of these under a single “Checkpoint” concept vs separate pages remains open.
6.2 Movement & Physics
Terrain is polygon/collider based, not tilemap‑locked.
Images, 9‑patch textures, and 3D objects may coexist as terrain.
Optional Sonic‑style physics can be enabled for specific characters.
7. Engine & Architecture Decisions
7.1 Actor System
Actors are the fundamental runtime unit.
Actor types include:
Character
Utility
Destructible
Trap
Item
Projectile
Four operational states:
Active
Semi‑Active
Passive
7.2 FSM System
FSM is inspired by OpenBOR.
State.gd  base class defines:
Group priorities
Context flags
State tags
Frame events

8. Tooling & Pipeline (Chat‑Only)
8.1 Wiki Strategy
Retraissance Wiki is the top‑level umbrella.
Densetsu Wiki is nested under Projects.
Densetsu is split into:
Universe Wiki (lore canon)
Engine Wiki (technical canon)
Automated page generation is the primary goal.
In‑browser editing is secondary but required.
8.2 Documentation Ingestion
Written information is prioritized over media.
Media extraction may fail gracefully with placeholders.
PDFs are acceptable archival sources; structure recovery is best‑effort.
9. Explicitly Open / Unresolved Items
These were identified during scaffold generation and discussion: - Canonical full names for Hanzo and
Hitomi. - Final hierarchy between Time Rifts and Anomalies. - Whether checkpoint mechanics should have a
shared parent concept page. - Final scope placement of Marella Caravans in main narrative timeline.
10. Usage Note
This document represents  chat‑level canon. It should be used to: - Validate generated wiki pages. -
Cross‑check Codex output. - Identify mismatches between formal documents and accepted decisions.
It should be updated whenever new project‑wide rules are decided in chat before document integration.
