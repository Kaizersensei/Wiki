Time Fan (Toki no Ōgi)

Overview

Legendary tessen infused with “Mugen” (infinity); parts veils between past, present, and possible futures. Bound to Hanzo as mortal bearer after Guardian relinquished it.

Known facts

  • Guardian of Eternity once wielded the fan; appears in echoes during temporal distortions.
  • Hanzo’s awakening with the fan triggers visions/tests; Okashira’s analysts fixate on its power.
  • Overuse can spawn Echo Worlds (e.g., Bimmy rift) and destabilize realms (Drowning Hour catalysts).
