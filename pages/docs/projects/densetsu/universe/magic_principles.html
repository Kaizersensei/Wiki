Canon Details

Time-linked magic shows up as anomalies, rifts, and regional “dead time” effects (e.g., Hirogawa loops, Seigetsu Phoenix Loop, Eresh Dream-State). Infinitree provides timeless energy; relic chambers and ancient symbols predate current languages. Use this page to collect rules for how magic/tech interacts with time fractures, biotech (Verdathal), buried machines (Eresh), and arcane relics (Galport, Seigetsu).
