Minus Depths

Overview

Glitched anomaly realm reminiscent of a “minus world”; flooded corridors with shifting walls, looping backgrounds, and water that slows time. Colors are muted with blue-pink corruption.

Hazards

  • Delayed movement and ghost afterimages; minimap static.
  • Neutral jellyfish guides; corrupted seaweed lashes with electric arcs.

Reward

Root Pearl hidden behind coral; surviving returns the player to the Infinitree with Echo Breath passive (water-zone agility boost).
