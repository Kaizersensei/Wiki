Kaerugan: The Iron Jungle

Overview

Techno-mystic industrial realm where nature and machine merge; trees hum with memory; entropy forges reclaim BE metal; home to Rustborn cults and guild oligarchs.

Known facts

  • Once vassal under King Frog; now Kaerugan Industrial Federation pushes independence.
  • Rustfall (metal rain) and Rust Bloom events mark entropy engine activity; Rustmoss thrives near foundries.
  • Hitomi left living code for Michio here; rumors of a fuel-less fire wielder.

Notable NPCs

  • Guild oligarchs / Rustborn leaders (local authority).
  • Industrial philosophers seeking chrono-tech recreation.
