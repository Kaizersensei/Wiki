Stats & Combat

Overview

Stats & Combat documents the combat runtime: hit detection, damage processing, projectile spawning, and camera hooks tied to combat states. Evidence comes from combat runtime scripts and projectile templates.

Scope

  • Hitbox / hurtbox handling
  • Damage and status effects
  • Attack state hooks and cancels
  • Projectile spawning and behavior
  • Combat-driven camera behaviors

Pages

  • Combat Overview — combat runtime and key systems.
  • Skills And Elemental Affinities — current evidence and open questions.
  • Stats Overview — stats data and integration points.

Status

Implemented. Combat scripts, projectile systems, and data directories are present in the evidence set.
