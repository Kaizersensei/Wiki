Actor Types & Lifecycle

Overview

Actor types share a common lifecycle defined by ActorInterface3D. Concrete actors (player, NPC, scenery) plug into these hooks to implement their behavior.

Lifecycle phases

  1. Initialize: initialize sets runtime references and prepares state.
  2. Ready: on_actor_ready runs after the actor enters the scene.
  3. Update: on_actor_update handles per-frame logic.
  4. Physics: on_actor_physics handles physics step logic.
  5. Hit: on_actor_hit handles damage reactions.
  6. Death: on_actor_death handles cleanup or state transitions.
  7. Respawn: on_actor_respawn restores actor state when applicable.

Actor types

  • ActorPlayer3D — player-controlled character actor.
  • ActorNPC3D — autonomous actor; includes wander phase helpers.
  • ActorScenery3D — non-character world actor.

Signals & state

  • actor_state_changed for state transitions.
  • actor_tag_added and actor_tag_removed for tag-driven logic.
  • Movement signals on ActorCharacter3D (landed, jumped, direction_changed, hurt).

Status

Implemented. Lifecycle hooks and concrete actor scenes are present.
