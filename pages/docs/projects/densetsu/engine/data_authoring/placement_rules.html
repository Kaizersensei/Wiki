Placement rules connect authored records to the conditions under which they may appear. They are the semantic bridge between Terrain3D substrate, biome meaning, topology masks, and deterministic generation output.

What Placement Rules Do

  • Choose what may spawn or be authored in a location.
  • Express conditions such as biome, topology, soil, moisture, slope, altitude, and exclusion zones.
  • Make generation explainable instead of opaque or random.
  • Keep placement logic separate from runtime state and separate from raw asset files.

Design Guardrails

  • Do not hide placement meaning inside hardcoded enums with no authoring surface.
  • Do not duplicate whole biome records just to express local placement changes.
  • Do not let runtime randomness replace deterministic authored rule evaluation.
  • Keep Terrain3D as substrate owner and use placement rules for semantic meaning above it.
