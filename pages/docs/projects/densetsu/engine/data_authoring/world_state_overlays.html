World-state overlays are authored conditions that modify normal content based on story, environment, temporal state, or region state. They do not replace the base catalog. They tell the engine which variants, suppressions, or extra rules should apply when a world condition is active.

Overlay Versus Variant

Concept Role
VariantThe alternate authored form, such as a burned bamboo or corrupted hound.
OverlayThe condition that decides when that alternate form replaces or modifies the normal one.
Runtime stateThe live per-instance state and save data, which should not be used as a substitute for overlay authoring.

Example Overlay Behaviors

Overlay pattern Typical effect
burnedReplace healthy foliage variants with burned variants and suppress some growth rules.
frozenSwap water or plant variants and change hazard or traversal expectations.
corruptedAdd corruption hazards, suppress sacred or healthy growth, and enable corrupted variants.
restoredRe-enable healthy variants and reduce hazard or enemy pressure.
time_sickAdd temporal anomalies and unstable props or effects.
occupiedInject camps, patrol anchors, barricades, or faction-specific actor sets.

Authoring Expectations

  • Overlays should reference variants and rule sets instead of duplicating whole biomes whenever possible.
  • Overlays should be able to replace, suppress, or add placement behavior.
  • Overlay activation should be explainable through story, temporal, environmental, or system triggers.
  • Compile and validation tooling should be able to preview the resolved result of an active overlay.
