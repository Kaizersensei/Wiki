Notes on Factions: 
●​ Kanmin is the new name for the predominant human culture. Kanmin (寒民) is a 
constructed term using kan (cold, frigid weather) and min (people/folk), which works 
for the "physically cold" interpretation of people who are bellic and distrustful. 
●​ The lore-correct term for humans in Densetsu is Suhada (Naked skin).

●​ Ocular Root – Sends remote sensory feedback to Michio. 
●​ Petal Projector – Projects illusionary reconstructions. 
●​ Sapdrip Totem – Detects nearby corruption. 
●​ Fracture Harp – Emits tones revealing anomaly proximity.​
