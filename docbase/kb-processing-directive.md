# KB Pull Workflow (Codex)

Use this whenever processing files from `docbase/kb/`.

1) **Discovery & Scoping**
   - List candidate wiki pages that could benefit from the KB file’s content (include slugs/paths).
   - Ask for confirmation to proceed before editing.

2) **Application**
   - When updating pages, follow the content-editor and sanitization directives (layout, tags, media, linking).
   - Propose illustration/graph slots using pseudotags (e.g., `<image>TODO-graph</image>` or a `<box>` note) where visuals would aid readers.

3) **Validation**
   - Keep content reader-facing; no editor artifacts.
   - Ensure links point to real pages; leave uncertain items unlinked and note under Notes.
