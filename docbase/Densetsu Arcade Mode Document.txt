Inspiration
Only striking features will be listed. Those relevant to us have a checkmark ✅.

* Mystic Warriors, Konami, Arcade
   * Up to 4 players ✅
   * 5 selectable characters ✅ perhaps even more
   * Frantic run n’ gun action ✅ but much less emphasis on it
   * Based on Sunset Warriors engine
   * Focus on shooting
   * Varied and interesting set pieces ✅
   * Minibosses ✅
   * Abilities - climbing walls, hanging, melee weapons, sliding, rolling ✅
   * Collectibles are scarce and predetermined - energy, points, power-up
   * Some auto-scroller or on-rails sections ✅
   * Levels can go in any direction with heavy use of walls and slopes ✅
   * Swimming as well as walking underwater ✅
   * Levels have some depth and the camera defines the plane of action ✅
   * Player plane is fixed (Pure 2D) ✅
   * Enemies have simple predefined movement routines ✅ but not all of them

* Streets of Rage Remake
   * Up to 2 Players
   * Polished brawler ✅
   * Can pick up items, but it’s optional ✅
   * Weapons can also be optionally picked up and can be blunt, sharp, firearms and bombs ✅
   * Player plane is 2.5D (Depth movement is faked with graphic and logic tricks)
   * Wide range of melee attacks and movement options ✅
   * Enemies have some basic judgement and have a “personality”
   * Varied and interesting set pieces, though few and far apart
   * Players have special attacks that need to recharge ✅
   * Clearing levels powers up your special attacks ✅
   * Losing lives diminishes your power ✅
   * Special attack power-ups can also be collected ✅
