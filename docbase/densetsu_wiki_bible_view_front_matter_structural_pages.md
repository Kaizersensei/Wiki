# Densetsu Wiki — Bible View
## Front Matter & Structural Pages Plan

This document defines **non-content pages** used only in the *Bible / Encyclopedic View*. These pages structure reading flow, establish tone, and create breathing space between major sections.

Media is embedded per page; no gallery or media-only pages are required.

---

## 1. Cover Page

**Purpose**  
Establish identity, tone, and scope. This is not an introduction, but a declaration.

**Content**
- Title: **DENSETSU**
- Subtitle: *A World Shaped by Time, Choice, and Consequence*
- Minimal emblem or sigil (Infinitree / Chronobonsai–derived)
- One short line of thematic text (e.g. a doctrine, proverb, or warning)

**Notes**
- No navigation
- No lore exposition
- Designed to be timeless and neutral

---

## 2. Reader’s Note / How to Read This Book

**Purpose**  
Set expectations for readers. Explain what this document is—and what it is not.

**Content**
- Clarify that this is:
  - a reference work
  - a myth-structured encyclopedia
  - not a linear story
- Explain layered truth:
  - myth vs record
  - memory vs timeline
- Encourage non-linear reading

**Notes**
- No spoilers
- No mechanics
- Calm, authoritative tone

---

## 3. Core Tenets Page (World Doctrine)

**Purpose**  
Anchor all subsequent reading in a shared philosophical foundation.

**Content**
- 5–7 short, declarative statements
  - e.g. rules about time, memory, power, legend
- Written as in-universe truths or observations

**Notes**
- Not attributed to a character
- Readable as law, proverb, or warning

---

## 4. Topic / Chapter Start Page (Reusable Template)

**Purpose**  
Act as a *threshold* between major sections (Cosmology, Factions, Characters, etc.).

**Content Template**
- Section title
- One short paragraph (2–4 lines) framing the theme
- Optional symbolic imagery

**Example Uses**
- “Cosmology & Time”
- “World & Geography”
- “Factions & Power”
- “People & Legends”

**Notes**
- No cross-links
- No lists
- Sets mood, not information density

---

## 5. Timeline Orientation Page

**Purpose**  
Prepare the reader before detailed events or arcs.

**Content**
- High-level explanation of:
  - BE / AE division
  - Great Eclipse as fixed point
  - Limited vs Full Agency eras
- Explicit statement of immutability (no retcons, no resets)

**Notes**
- No dates
- No events
- Conceptual only

---

## 6. Terminology & Symbols Primer

**Purpose**  
Reduce cognitive load for new readers.

**Content**
- Short explanations of recurring terms:
  - Anomaly
  - Concord
  - Eclipse-bound
  - World Memory
- Visual symbols or glyphs if used consistently

**Notes**
- Not a full lexicon (that exists elsewhere)
- Focused on *frequently recurring* concepts

---

## 7. Interlude / Breathing Pages (Optional, Repeatable)

**Purpose**  
Prevent fatigue in dense sections.

**Content Options**
- Short myth fragment
- Anonymous quote
- Fragmented record
- Sensory description (place, sound, texture)

**Notes**
- No hard lore
- No names required
- Used sparingly between large sections

---

## 8. Appendix Divider Pages

**Purpose**  
Clearly separate narrative/lore material from technical reference.

**Content**
- Simple statement such as:
  > “What follows describes how the world is built, not how it is lived.”

**Notes**
- Used once before Engine / Technical sections

---

## Summary

These pages:
- frame the encyclopedia as a *world artifact*, not a wiki
- guide readers without overwhelming them
- preserve mystery while enforcing consistency

They should remain stable even as content pages expand or change.
