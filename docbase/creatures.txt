### Creatures — comprehensive list with cross-reference

*(compiled strictly from project chats, memories, and the **Enemies, Creatures, Abominations** document references; no inferred additions)*

---

#### **Cave Rat**

* **Description:** Small subterranean scavenger inhabiting caves, mines, and underground passages. Operates in groups, relies on instinct, and reinforces low-level ecosystem activity.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor (instinct-driven FSM).
* **Interactions:** Can be hunted, studied, or culled; not narrative-driven.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Cave fauna / early-game creatures.

---

#### **Hagane Snail**

* **Description:** Slow-moving gastropod with a hardened, metal-like shell adapted to mineral-rich environments. Extremely durable for its size.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor; high defense, low mobility.
* **Interactions:** Environmental hazard, resource source, or study target.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Armored / mineral creatures.

---

#### **Bamboo Creatures (family)**

* **Description:** Plant-based fauna native to bamboo forests. Exist in multiple variants with different behaviors (passive, territorial, reactive), but all remain ecological rather than sentient enemies.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor; biome-bound logic.
* **Interactions:** Can be avoided, hunted, studied, or interacted with indirectly through quests tied to bamboo regions.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Bamboo / plant creatures.

---

#### **Forest Fauna (general group)**

* **Description:** Non-unique wildlife populating forested regions (e.g., deer-like, boar-like, spirit-adjacent animals). Emphasize atmosphere and ecological balance rather than combat challenge.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor; ambient or reactive AI.
* **Interactions:** Hunting, observation, environmental storytelling.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Forest fauna category.

---

#### **Aquatic Fauna (general group)**

* **Description:** Non-humanoid marine and freshwater creatures inhabiting rivers, coasts, and deep-sea regions. Separate from sentient aquatic factions.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor with water-specific movement logic.
* **Interactions:** Optional hunting, study, traversal context.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Aquatic creatures.

---

#### **Desert Fauna (general group)**

* **Description:** Creatures adapted to arid regions such as the Ashen Sands near Eresh and Kaerugan. Often burrowing, nocturnal, or heat-resistant.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor; environment-adaptive behaviors.
* **Interactions:** Environmental hazards, resource gathering, ecological reinforcement.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Desert creatures.

---

#### **Mycomind Fauna**

* **Description:** Fungus-associated creatures native to the Mycomind Covenant. Behavior often symbiotic or parasitic, tied to spores, decay, and growth cycles.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor; fungal/ecological logic.
* **Interactions:** Study, taming, or environmental manipulation.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Fungal / Mycomind creatures.

---

#### **Mount-Class Creatures**

* **Description:** Larger fauna suitable for riding or transport. Defined by traversal utility rather than combat role.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor with mount/riding extensions.
* **Interactions:** Taming, bonding, traversal mechanics.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Mounts / rideable creatures.

---

#### **Corrupted Creatures (state, not a species)**

* **Description:** Any creature temporarily altered by corruption, anomalies, or environmental sickness. Behavior becomes hostile and enemy-like while corrupted.
* **Systems:** Creature Actor with corruption overlay; does **not** become an Enemy Actor.
* **Interactions:** Combat, cleansing, or containment; may revert to normal state.
* **Cross-reference:** *Enemies, Creatures, Abominations* — Corruption states.

---

### Enemies

* 
**Bandit**: Attacking any enemy humanoid or any hostile regardless of alignment or skill. 


* 
**Ninja Grunts**: They belong to factions that are antagonistic to the player and act out of duty or spite. 


* 
**Okashira**: A main antagonist boss or sub-boss who is unique and key to the game’s events. 



### Creatures (Lore Fauna)

* 
**Grizzleback**: A territorial bear-like beast that mutates from overconsumption of cursed berries. 


* 
**Chitter Vineserpent**: A carnivorous plant-animal hybrid that hunts by mimicking the sounds of crying children. 


* 
**Mire Crawler**: A giant, slime-covered insect with relentless hunger. 


* 
**Wyrm Pup**: The abandoned young of a mountain dragon that lashes out due to trauma. 


* 
**Shard Beetle**: Explodes into shards of crystal on death, damaging everything nearby. 


* 
**Mist Hounds**: Creatures made of condensed fog used by a shadow clan to scent-track targets. 


* 
**Lantern Moths**: Serve forest monks by marking intruders with glowing spores. 


* 
**Sand Whiptail**: Domesticated by desert warlords as agile mounts and weapons. 


* 
**Celadon Cranes**: Peaceful guardians of sacred ponds that attack if the waters are touched. 


* 
**Mossbound Goats**: Harmless protectors that become furious if provoked near shrines. 


* 
**The Hollow Fang**: A wolf-fox hybrid that devoured its own spirit to protect its kin and now hunts the impure. 


* 
**Aurex the Last Basilisk**: The final of its kind, guarding a vault of cursed knowledge. 


* 
**Komo the Earth Dreamer**: A temple-sized sleeping turtle that awakens during celestial misalignment. 


* 
**Shyr'aani the Skybreaker**: A thunderbird spirit seeking release or revenge against the monks who imprisoned it. 


* 
**The River Crown**: A koi dragon cursed to devour those who steal from sacred waters.

**Echo of the First Betrayer**: A cursed wind that whispers treachery.
