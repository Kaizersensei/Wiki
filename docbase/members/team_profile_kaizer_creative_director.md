## Profile

Architect of the **Densetsu** universe and founding force behind **Retraissance**. Kaizer Sensei operates at the intersection of narrative design, systems thinking, and artistic direction. Since 2012, he has shaped Densetsu not as a single game, but as a living world governed by internal rules, moral laws, and long‑term consequences.

Rather than chasing trends, his work emphasizes **cohesion, restraint, and intent** — designing systems that reinforce theme instead of undermining it.

---

- Oversees lore consistency across game, comics, and future media
- Designs high‑level gameplay philosophy and systemic constraints
- Reviews major mechanics for narrative alignment
- Coordinates between art, code, and story
