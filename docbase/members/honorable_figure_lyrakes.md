# Lyrakes

*Honorable Figure / Advocate*

---

## Title
**Lyrakes**

## Standing
Honorable Figure · Project Advocate · Hardware Patron

## Domain
Material support, advocacy, goodwill

---

## Profile

Lyrakes is not a member of the Retraissance core team, but an **honorable figure** whose support has had a tangible impact on the project’s ability to move forward.

Rather than contributing through direct development work, Lyrakes supports Densetsu by providing **material resources**, encouragement, and trust — enabling the team to focus on creation rather than survival.

His involvement is rooted in belief in the project’s vision and long-term potential, not in authority, ownership, or creative control.

---

## Role in the Project

- Donates and facilitates access to hardware and equipment
- Acts as an external advocate through word-of-mouth publicity
- Vouches for the project’s seriousness and integrity
- Provides moral support during critical development phases

---

## Relationship to Retraissance

Lyrakes holds **no decision-making power** over creative, technical, or narrative direction.

His role is one of:
- trust
- endorsement
- material backing

This relationship is based on mutual respect and shared values rather than contractual obligation.

---

## Significance

Support figures like Lyrakes represent an often invisible but essential part of independent creative work:

- belief before proof
- support before success
- contribution without expectation of control

---

## Alignment

**Ethos Axis:** Generosity ↔ Integrity  
**Support Axis:** Advocacy ↔ Stability

---

## Notes

Lyrakes’ contributions help anchor the project in reality, reminding the team that external belief exists even before public recognition.
