# Kodomaru — Reactor Key & Eresh Expedition Addendum

## Context

This addendum links **Kodomaru** directly to the Technogarch reactor crisis and formalizes his role in the Eresh arc as both a **mechanical necessity** and a **mythic continuity anchor**.

---

## Kodomaru’s Function

Kodomaru is the **only viable interface** capable of surviving proximity to the Mirror Spire’s ruined reactor core.

His unique wood composition possesses:
- extreme resistance to heat and radiation-like energies,
- natural insulation against temporal distortion,
- adaptive resonance with corrupted Technogarch machinery.

This is not immunity.
It is *tolerance*.

---

## Pip Integration Phase (Gameplay + Lore)

During the Eresh expedition phase:

- **Pip must wear Kodomaru as living armor**.
- Kodomaru partially unfolds, encasing Pip in a reinforced wooden exoskeleton.
- Movement becomes heavier, deliberate, and forceful.

Gameplay expression:
- Tank-like momentum
- High impact landings
- Limited agility compensated by **frog-like leaps**, ground slams, and environmental traversal
- Environmental hazards become survivable, not harmless

Kodomaru does not act as a vehicle.
He acts as **a burden willingly carried**.

---

## Reactor Interaction

Kodomaru’s body resonates with the remaining Technogarch systems.

When brought near the reactor remnants:
- dormant mechanisms reactivate briefly,
- sealed pathways unlock,
- stabilization sequences become possible.

Kodomaru does not repair the reactor directly.
He allows **safe approach, access, and choice**.

---

## Origin of Compatibility (Mythic Layer)

Ancient hieroglyph-like records discovered in Eresh depict:

- a **strange man** offering a *giant tree-shaped being* to a pharaonic ruler,
- the figure standing unharmed before unbearable light and heat,
- the tree-man acting as both shield and offering.

These records are fragmented, symbolic, and never explicit.

They predate known Technogarch collapse timelines.

---

## Interpretive Implications

The records suggest:
- Kodomaru’s kind was known, feared, or revered long before the Eclipse,
- the Technogarch did not invent this solution — they **borrowed or attempted to replicate it**,
- their failure came from imitation without understanding.

Whether the strange man was:
- the Bonsai Sage,
- a predecessor,
- or a temporal echo

is intentionally left unresolved.

---

## Sensory Lore Integration

Rather than direct exposition, Kodomaru’s significance is conveyed through:
- heat distortion bending away from his surface,
- hostile machinery falling silent near him,
- environmental audio dampening when he fully deploys,
- visual motifs echoing the hieroglyphs in reactor chambers.

The world *recognizes* him.

---

## Thematic Alignment

Kodomaru reinforces core Densetsu themes:

- survival through coexistence, not domination
- protection through life, not steel
- the cost of carrying what others abandoned

He is not a key forged for a lock.

He is proof that the lock was never meant to be forced.

---

## Canon Status

- Kodomaru is essential to the Eresh arc.
- His armor phase is mandatory, not optional.
- His existence predates the Technogarch collapse.
- His role is hinted, never fully explained.

Mystery is preserved by design.
