# Yama Binder

## Summary
Skeletal dog bound by soul-fire chains, loyal only to the truly cursed. Hunts targets marked by curses/sin, dragging them toward soul pyres; ignores the untainted. Chains can be broken with blunt/holy attacks; pyres can be doused to weaken its fire. A heavier curse can briefly leash or redirect it.

## Known facts
- Described as “skeletal dog bound by chains of soul fire, loyal only to the truly cursed.”
- Prioritizes cursed targets; uses chain drags and doom-howls.
- Weakness levers: chain breaking, pyre extinguishing, curse-cleansing.
