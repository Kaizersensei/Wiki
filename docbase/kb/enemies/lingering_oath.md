# Lingering Oath

## Summary
Oathbound spirit knight guarding a forgotten vow; replays echoes of recent combat to punish repetition. Duelist encounter tied to a vow circle of sigils.

## Known facts
- Echoes last 3 player moves; breaking sigils forces a vulnerable state.
- Weakness: disrupt vow circle (sigils); repetition gets countered.
