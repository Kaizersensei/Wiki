# Gilded Ghost

## Summary
Faceless tomb-ward assassin bound to reflective metal and light. Mirrors player stances/weapons; strikes from reflections and metal surfaces. Weak to disrupting reflections (dust, acid, shatter) or severing its light-binding.

## Known facts
- Haunts royal tomb robbers; adapts to player loadout.
- Weakness: disrupt reflection/light binding.
