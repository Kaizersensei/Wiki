# Rotling Mass

## Summary
Cursed pile of meat that mimics the dead it devours. Shambles toward corpses, absorbs them, and copies their moveset in a sloppy, slowed form. Core weak points glow during digestion; fire, light, and cleansing salts shrink or halt it. Appears where burial customs are broken; respectful rites can suppress spawns.

## Known facts
- Described as “pile of cursed meat that mimics the dead it devours.”
- Splits off smaller meatlings if left unchecked; leaves slowing rot trails.
- Weak to cleansing elements (fire/light/salt) and to interruption during digestion.
