# Mirrorspawn

## Summary
Warped, backwards copy of the player that despises all living beings. Spawns in mirror/illusion spaces, copies current loadout with corrupted variants, and attacks from reversed angles. Cannot heal; shatters when forced into reflective surfaces or when symmetry is broken. Beating it can realign a room or reveal hidden paths.

## Known facts
- Defined as “copies your form, but backwards and warped; despises all living beings.”
- Mirrors player/leader loadout at spawn; attacks from opposite guard side.
- Weak to reflection/symmetry breaks (mirrors, uneven terrain, asymmetrical buffs).
