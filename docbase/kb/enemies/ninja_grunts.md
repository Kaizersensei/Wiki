# Ninja Grunts

## Summary
Hostile ninja foot soldiers acting from duty or spite. Rotate between shuriken volleys and short blade rushes; deploy smokescreens to reposition (smoke is flammable and dispersible by wind). Morale breaks if leaders fall or supply lines are cut. Often tied to Kojimigakure-linked cells or hired as ambushers.

## Known facts
- Described as factional enemies acting out of duty/spite.
- Toolkit: light blades, shuriken, smoke; brittle morale under pressure.
- Ambush roles in forests/rooftops; sparing captives could yield intel routes.
