# Ashen Kite

## Summary
Phoenix-like paper-and-flame kite; ignites to dive-bomb and can be reborn when written about. Weak to water/wind disruption; stopping its “script” ends the loop.

## Known facts
- Attacks with flaming dives, creates ash/draft hazards.
- Respawns tied to narrative inscription (scrolls, records); neutralize by sealing or destroying the script.
- Weak to water and shredding wind.
