# Threadworms

## Summary
Parasitic spirits that stitch into corpses and puppet them chaotically. Hosts attack with jerky, unpredictable timing; if denied hosts, the worms swarm as a crawling cloud that slows and infects. Fire, consecrated light, salt, or strong wind/cold scatters or burns the threads. Outbreaks signal failed burials or atrocity sites.

## Known facts
- Described as parasitic spirits that puppeteer corpses.
- Vulnerable to fire/light/salt; wind or cold can scatter a loose swarm.
- Outbreaks tied to neglected dead (mass graves, battlefields).
