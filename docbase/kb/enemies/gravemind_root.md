# Gravemind Root

## Summary
Rot/decay growth born from sacrilegious burial grounds. Spreads roots/spores to create decay zones that debuff healing/armor and summon lesser rotlings. Weak to fire/purification and sanctified ground.

## Known facts
- Corrupts ground, spreading rot; summons lesser rotlings.
- Decay zones reduce healing/armor effectiveness.
- Weakness: fire/purification; sanctify ground to contain it.
