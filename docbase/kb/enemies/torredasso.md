# Torredasso

## Summary
Giant Manueline-inspired mech relic (Belém-tower silhouette, filigree, armillary joints, funicular winch mobility). Treated as a relic-class construct rather than a living enemy; can be repaired and recruited as an ally after a side quest. When restored, Torredasso can serve under Don Bastion’s reformed army (Saul Azar’s supporters were tried and sentenced).

## Known facts
- Built as an armored construct; weak points likely at armillary/bearing joints.
- Vertical winch suggests arenas with elevation control.
- Can return as an ally if repaired; becomes part of Don Bastion’s reformed forces.
