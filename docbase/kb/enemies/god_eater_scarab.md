# God Eater Scarab

## Summary
Scarab bloated with divine bile after devouring part of a deity. Light/Dark mixed bile; targets relics/shrines and heals by consuming blessed items. Weakness when forced to overeat or when wards trigger bile backlash.

## Known facts
- Emits corrosive light/dark bile bursts.
- Seeks relic energy; heals from blessed items.
- Weakness: bile backfire via overfeeding or relic wards.
