# Forgotten Shell

## Summary
Colossal tortoise bearing an ecosystem; wakes once a century. Earth/Water aligned; movement reshapes terrain and can crush regions. Weak to calming/grounding rites and limb diversion.

## Known facts
- Carries flora/fauna on its shell; dormant shell can be explored.
- Awakening causes localized quakes and crushing paths.
- Slow but unstoppable when roused; path memory over centuries.
