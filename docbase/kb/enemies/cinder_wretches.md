# Cinder Wretches

## Summary
Burned souls that sprint endlessly, igniting anything they touch. Fire/entropy-tainted; weak to water/wards. Often tied to arson/accident sites (e.g., Hirogawa fire).

## Known facts
- Endless runners; contact inflicts burn and leaves flaming paths.
- Water/warding slows or extinguishes them; chokepoints mitigate spread.
- Narrative hook: cleansing rites at origin sites may calm or release them.
