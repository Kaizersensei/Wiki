# World Overview

The world of Densetsu is a fractured yet living system shaped by the Great Eclipse.
It is not a globe of uniform history, but a collection of regions bound together by
shared consequence rather than shared origin.

## Core Traits
- Time behaves inconsistently across regions
- Geography reflects historical and elemental scars
- Cultural memory varies by location

No region exists in isolation; all are affected, directly or indirectly, by temporal instability.
