# The Crimson Courts

## Alignment
**Lawful Authoritarian / Utilitarian Control**

The Courts prioritize stability and perception of order over truth, justice, or individual life.

---

## Structure

* Multi-member masked judiciary
* Judges act independently but escalate collectively
* Masks represent roles (Dragon, Ox, Snake, etc.), not persons
* Authority is fragmented by design, preventing accountability

---

## Methods of Control

* Public ritual (Arena) for legitimacy
* Quiet inspections for compliance
* Fire as controlled displacement
* Accusations as regulatory tools, not moral judgments

---

## Known Figures

* **Masked Judges**  
  Anonymous, role-bound authorities enforcing doctrine.

* **Arena Champion (Yǒng Lóng Quán)**  
  Instrument of spectacle and containment, not ideology.

* **Court Soldiers**  
  Disciplined enforcers trained for procedure over initiative.

---

## Doctrine vs. Reality

**Public Doctrine:**  
Boiling salt water is alchemy.

**Practical Reality:**  
The Courts already know the river is compromised.  
Potable water is being stockpiled elsewhere and rationed silently.

---

## Relationship to the Ninth Wave

* Courts pay heavy “immunity taxes” to avoid direct interference
* Resource shortages are partially caused by Ninth Wave coercion
* This arrangement is intentionally unspoken
* Revealed later in connection with the revolution

The Courts do not rule alone. They manage pressure.
