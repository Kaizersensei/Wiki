# Addendum: The Physical Eclipse and the Sage Intervention

## Revised Canonical Sequence

The Great Eclipse was **physically real**.

The sun and the moon were locked in place for an extended period, halting natural cycles and pushing the world toward ecological collapse. Life withered without moving sunlight. The seas flattened without lunar pull. This state persisted long enough to scar the planet permanently.

---

## The Chronobonsai Era (Centuries Before Year 0)

Long before the Eclipse ended, **Chronobonsai trees** began to take root across the world.

These trees were not planted as monuments, but as **temporal anchors**:
- each Chronobonsai recorded local causality,
- absorbed lived memory,
- and stabilized micro-flows of time in its surrounding region.

At this stage, their purpose was unknown to most cultures.

---

## Arrival of the Bonsai Sage — Year 0

At **Year 0**, the Bonsai Sage appeared.

He had foreseen the next **1,500 years of this timeline**, on the condition that he acted at the correct moment.

His doctrine was simple and absolute:

> Time must be left alone — but it can be *anchored*.

---

## The Infinitree Event

In Year 0, the **Infinitree** emerged.

It grew from the **combined life force of all existing Chronobonsai trees**.

- The Chronobonsais were drained and died.
- Their accumulated memories were assimilated.
- The Infinitree became a singular, planetary anchor.

This act:
- reignited the stale flow of time,
- restored motion to the sun and moon,
- and resolved the astronomical lock caused by the Technogarchs.

The Eclipse ended.

The damage did not.

---

## The Technogarch Collapse

At the exact moment the Infinitree stabilized the timeline, the Technogarch civilization reached its breaking point.

Their cities of metal, built for:
- extreme population density,
- massive energy throughput,
- and unsustainable food demand,

were dependent on the **Mirror Spire**.

The Spire’s core imploded.

---

## The Mirror Spire Implosion

The implosion created a **localized black hole** at the Spire’s core.

The Spire itself was constructed from a material capable of withstanding virtually infinite force. It did not collapse.

Reality around it did.

Consequences included:
- landslides on a continental scale,
- mountains splitting apart,
- entire landmasses inverting or overturning,
- permanent geological distortions.

---

## Aftermath: Eresh

As the Infinitree eased the temporal instability, the **gravitational pull weakened**.

But the damage was irreversible.

The Technogarchs were buried alive along with their cities, knowledge, and records beneath the transformed land.

This region would later be known as **Eresh**.

Though the black hole’s pull diminished, its core **continues to emit extreme heat**, defining the Ashen Sands and volcanic mills of the region.

---

## Canonical Outcome

- The Eclipse ends in Year 0.
- Time resumes, but remains wounded.
- The Technogarch civilization is erased.
- The Infinitree stands as the sole planetary anchor.

The world survives — not because time was controlled,
but because it was finally allowed to move again.
