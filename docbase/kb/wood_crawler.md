# Wood Crawler

## Classification
Creature — Arboreal Ambush Predator

## Overview
The Wood Crawler is a reptilian–arboreal hybrid whose body mimics dead or living trees with unsettling accuracy. Its skin resembles layered bark stretched over powerful muscle, and its face is sealed beneath a hardened wooden growth that forms naturally from the skull itself. Motionless for long periods, it is often mistaken for part of the forest until it strikes.

Wood Crawlers embody the danger of unmanaged wilderness: patient, self-sustaining, and violently indifferent.

## Anatomy
- **Body:** Reptile-like musculature with elongated limbs and a prehensile tail adapted for climbing and anchoring.
- **Skin:** Stratified bark plates with deep cracks exposing fibrous tissue beneath.
- **Face Growth:** A natural cranial bark formation, not a worn mask; hollow eye cavities obscure intent and scent.
- **Limbs:** Long, hook-like claws capable of gripping bark, stone, and flesh.

## Locomotion
- Capable of **rapid quadrupedal running**, despite an upright resting posture.
- Excels at **leaping, climbing, and lateral pouncing**.
- Rarely pursues prey over long distances; relies on burst movement and terrain advantage.

## Behavior
- Strictly solitary and territorial; attacks its own kind on sight.
- Hunts through static ambush, triggered by vibration and movement rather than sight.
- Uses mass and leverage to pin or crush prey before delivering fatal blows.

## Reproduction Cycle
Wood Crawlers reproduce endlessly, but only one offspring is produced per death.
- Upon death, the creature drops a **seedling core**.
- If not destroyed within minutes, the seedling feeds on the rotting corpse and rapidly matures.
- Fire is the only reliable way to interrupt this cycle.

## Elemental Interactions
- **Fire:** Severe weakness; halts regeneration and destroys seedlings.
- **Ice / Water:** Mild weakness; susceptible to frostbite and sog states.
- **Earth:** Mildly strengthening.
- **Gaea:** Strongly strengthening; accelerates regeneration and bark hardening.

## Legendary Variant — Elder Wood Crawler
Rare, ancient specimens grown massive and overburdened with age.
- **Traits:** Enormous bulk, slower movement, extreme impact force.
- **Tactics:** Precision pounces; relies on weight to kill prey on impact rather than pursuit.
- **Appearance:** Covered in moss, vines, fungi, and secondary flora; bark cracks house entire micro-ecosystems.

Elder Wood Crawlers are often described as the **inverse of Kodomaru**—not guardians of balance, but proof of nature’s capacity to overgrow and consume unchecked.

## Narrative Role
- Environmental threat rather than a corrupted entity.
- Reinforces themes of camouflage, patience, and lethal stillness in forest regions.
- Teaches players to read terrain carefully and respect old-growth wilderness.
