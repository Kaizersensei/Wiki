# Principles of Magic

Magic in Densetsu is not an external force to be wielded freely, but a **resonant interaction** between living beings, elements, and the world’s temporal state.

## Core Laws
- Magic is **contextual**: its behavior depends on place, time phase, and elemental balance.
- Magic has **cost**: physical, mental, or environmental.
- Magic cannot violate temporal stability; attempts to do so create anomalies.

## Sources
- Elemental concords
- Living ecosystems
- Artifacts and ancient constructs

Magic is shaped, not commanded.
