# The Infinitree

The Infinitree is a trans-temporal organism anchoring reality after the Eclipse.

## Properties
- Roots extend across time layers
- Acts as a stabilizer for anomalies
- Cannot be controlled or replicated
- Maintains a Timeline Log: an unbiased record of events as they happened (contrasts with World Memory, which stores sealed individual perspectives)

It represents endurance rather than dominance over time.
