NINTH WAVE: COERCION, RIVER ANOMALY, AND “IMMUNITY TAXES”
(Canon placement: revealed in connection with revolution)

Narrative status / placement
- The Ninth Wave’s presence is intentionally “lost” or muted after Hirogawa.
- Their involvement returns as a revelation tied to the developing revolution, not earlier.

Core involvement (agreed)
- Shortages and rationing in Crimson lands are partly due to Ninth Wave coercion.
- There exists a system of high “mafia taxes” paid by the Courts in exchange for “immunity.”
- These arrangements were not openly spoken of; they are revealed as the revolution is underway.

What “immunity” means (usable interpretation without over-specifying)
- Protection from targeted disruption, sabotage, or escalation that would otherwise be attributed to “anomaly,” “alchemy,” or unrest.
- A stabilizing payment that still starves the population—because resource extraction and stockpiling remain aligned to power survival, not public welfare.

Relationship to the river anomaly (agreed direction, minimal specifics)
- The river anomaly (salt intrusion / catastrophe) is a pressure point.
- It functions both as material scarcity and as a narrative lever for dependency control.
- The Ninth Wave can exploit this condition to extract tribute, intensify dependency, or destabilize court legitimacy.

Reveal strategy (tone-consistent)
- Avoid speeches and villain monologues.
- Reveal through:
  - documents/seals seen at the wrong time
  - whispered logistics (“stockpile routes”)
  - abrupt policy shifts that suddenly “make sense”
  - a pattern of “paid calm” that fails once payments falter
