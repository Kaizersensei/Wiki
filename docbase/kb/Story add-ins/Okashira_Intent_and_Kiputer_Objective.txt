OKASHIRA: INTENT AND KIPUTER OBJECTIVE
(Canon intent framing; avoids cliché “world domination/time control”)

Okashira’s goal (agreed framing)
- Okashira wants the Kiputer inside Mirror Spire.
- Not to “predict war outcomes” for tactical advantage.
- Purpose: find the right conditions for a new Great Eclipse (a world reset) where Okashira becomes the one who binds time (instead of the Sage).

Self-concept
- Okashira does not see himself as a villain.
- He frames himself as the correcting hand of destiny: the agent who restores coherence when the world becomes unstable through divergence.

Mechanism: coherence via agency compression
- Okashira’s worldview treats widespread agency as “noise” that creates divergence/branches.
- Branches threaten the conditions required for successful rebinding.
- Therefore, the plan is not “rule the future,” but reduce agency until rebinding becomes inevitable.

Operational posture (timeline control without “omniscient mastermind” tone)
- Okashira can be positioned as near/at the Kiputer throughout, influencing events indirectly.
- The unfolding land-by-land outcomes can be “more or less controlled,” not perfectly.
- Michio’s lived choices create branches that threaten Okashira’s desired outcome timeline.
- This explains why Okashira intervenes personally near the end: not for spectacle, but because the branch-risk becomes unacceptable.

Design constraints implied by this framing
- Avoid melodramatic “I control everything” exposition.
- Show system behaviors that compress choice:
  - scarcity management
  - procedural accusations
  - spectacle justice when needed
  - anti-knowledge enforcement
- Let the reader infer intent through consequences rather than declarations.
