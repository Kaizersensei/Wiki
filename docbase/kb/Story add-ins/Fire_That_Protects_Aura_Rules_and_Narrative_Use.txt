THE FIRE THAT PROTECTS: AURA RULES AND NARRATIVE USE
(Canon constraints and intent)

What it is (agreed)
- Not an aggressive “projected shield expanding radius of defense” as an intentional combat tool.
- It remains (for now) an unintentional fire-reactive aura.

What it does in practice (usable behaviors)
- Neutralizes / absorbs / redirects hostile fire Ki rather than “casting” fire.
- Under stress, growth of the aura can cause an unintentional heat-blast:
  - can disrupt formations
  - can throw attackers back
  - can create crowd movement opportunities (e.g., doors forced open, formation broken)

What it must NOT become (constraints)
- No proud heroic “chosen one” framing.
- No “destiny” monologues tied to it.
- Avoid it becoming a clean, repeatable superpower with explicit rules too early.
- Keep it experiential: consequence-first, explanation-later (or never fully explained).

Narrative role (agreed direction)
- Serves as a mechanism for escape dynamics without turning the scene into power fantasy.
- The aura’s presence shifts how institutions react (fear upward, protocol breaks).
- It should be legible through outcomes, not named systems.
