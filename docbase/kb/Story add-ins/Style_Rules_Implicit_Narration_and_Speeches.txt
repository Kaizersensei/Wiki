STYLE RULES: IMPLICIT NARRATION AND WHEN SPEECHES HAPPEN
(Agreed authorial principles)

Core principle
- Most meaning is implied.
- Readers follow threads; they are not handed conclusions.
- Information is presented as “strings,” with only the tip visible most of the time.

Speeches / exposition usage (agreed)
- Speeches (and exposition) happen only when they must:
  - to convey someone’s exact thoughts and message
  - to preserve character voice and intent
- Otherwise: show via consequence, behavior, environment, procedure.

Environmental pacing (agreed)
- After high-action sequences, allow a breather:
  - ambient sections
  - detachment
  - consequences rather than recap
- Control cognitive load:
  - section length discipline
  - imagery-first passages that remain translatable into mental images without infodumping

Protagonist decentering (agreed)
- It is valid (and desired) to “forget Michio for a while”:
  - everyone looks for him
  - he wants to be forgotten (in that place, by those people)
- Protagonists require a world that supports the journey; the world can lead for long stretches.
