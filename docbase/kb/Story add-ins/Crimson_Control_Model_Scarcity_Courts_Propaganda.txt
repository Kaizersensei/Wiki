CRIMSON CONTROL MODEL: SCARCITY, COURTS, AND PROPAGANDA
(Canon mechanics; built for implicit storytelling)

Principle
- Control is maintained less by omnipresent violence and more by engineered dependency:
  - water access
  - fear currency
  - knowledge restriction
  - spectacle justice when needed

“Alchemy” as a procedural accusation (agreed)
- “Boiling salt water is alchemy” is public doctrine.
- In practice, it’s a witch hunt where there are no witches:
  - accusation is flexible
  - used to regulate fear and dependency
  - criminalizes knowledge circulation (“how to survive without the state”)

Water monitoring behavior (agreed beats)
- Guards prioritize wells first:
  - check water levels before checking people
  - ration quietly
  - enforce “protocol” behaviorally (administrative tone, not fanaticism)
- Authorities already know the river is compromised.
- The “crime” is bypassing dependency, not the act of boiling.

Propaganda posture after destabilization (agreed)
- When searches fail but rumors persist, the regime claims:
  - the Court “won” (regardless of reality)
  - speaking otherwise is treason (execution)
- Purpose: last-attempt control of narrative space and fear.

Professionalism vs fragility (agreed trajectory)
- Early: guards appear competent/professional.
- As authority erodes (patrols disappear; conscription denied), their training gaps surface:
  - cowering behavior
  - reliance on symbolic authority
  - poor adaptation under mass noncompliance
