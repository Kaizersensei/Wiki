# Crimson Culture

## Visual Identity
Crimson culture emphasizes hierarchy and inevitability.

* Dominant colors: crimson, black lacquer, muted gold
* Layered robes with exaggerated silhouettes
* Masks signify function, not identity
* Architecture leans inward, framing authority as omnipresent

---

## Linguistic Hooks
Language avoids agency and responsibility.

* Common abstractions:
  * “Adjustment”
  * “Correction”
  * “Rebalancing”
* Accusations framed as conditions, not crimes
  * “Alchemy”
  * “Disruption”
  * “Irregular behavior”

Speech implies necessity rather than choice.

---

## Behavioral Norms

* Direct questions are avoided
* Compliance is visible, internalization is expected
* Fear is habitual, not panicked
* Silence is safer than denial

Civilians learn to survive by predicting enforcement rather than resisting it.

---

## Cultural Tensions

* Survival practices become crimes when noticed
* Innovation without sanction is labeled heresy
* Visibility equals liability

---

## Core Belief
Order is not moral—it is **necessary**.  
Those who disrupt it are not enemies, but errors to be corrected.
