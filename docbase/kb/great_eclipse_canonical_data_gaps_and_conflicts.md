# The Great Eclipse

## 1. Canonical Definition
The **Great Eclipse** is the singular, non-repeating cosmological rupture that defines all modern chronology in *Densetsu*. It is not a conventional astronomical eclipse, but a multi-layered event affecting **time, elements, memory, and causality** simultaneously.

It functions as:
- The **absolute temporal anchor** of the world
- The boundary between mythic history and recorded history
- The origin point of all modern temporal instability

All post-Eclipse dating is measured relative to this event.

---

## 2. Temporal Classification

### Epoch Split
- **BE (Before Eclipse)**: Mythic, unreliable, partially overwritten time
- **AE (After Eclipse)**: Stabilized but damaged timeline

The Eclipse itself is **non-durational**: it is treated as an instant that nonetheless caused effects across centuries.

Conversion between BE and AE is explicitly impossible.

---

## 3. Simultaneous Canonical Effects

At the peak instant of the Great Eclipse, the following events are known to have occurred **at once**, despite geographic and causal impossibility:

- **All elemental forces halted simultaneously**
- **Light dimmed globally**, independent of local geography
- **The Infinitree rooted itself across time layers**, acting as a stabilizing anchor
- **The Bonsai Sage appeared**, arriving from a ruined future timeline
- **The Fire Emperor fell**, ending the old elemental order
- **Yukigami froze entirely**, encasing the Hyōken clan in suspended time

These events are treated as co-equal consequences, not a causal chain.

---

## 4. Physical & Metaphysical Consequences

### World-Level Effects
- Creation of **temporal rifts and anomalies**
- Introduction of **looped regions and frozen zones**
- Permanent instability in time flow consistency

### Ontological Effects
- Time becomes an **ecosystem**, not a linear force
- Cause and effect may invert or echo
- Some entities persist across multiple temporal states

---

## 5. The Eclipse as Design Law

A hard narrative and systemic rule emerges from the Eclipse:

> **Time is not a tool.**

Implications:
- No direct player-controlled time travel
- Temporal manipulation exists only via:
  - Anomalies
  - Rifts
  - Narrative constraints
- Attempted domination of time results in corruption or collapse

This principle underpins:
- Chronogardening
- World Phases
- Loop Systems
- Anomaly Resolution

---

## 6. Cultural & Historical Interpretation

### Consensus View (AE Societies)
- The Eclipse marks the end of the Old World
- It is treated as a catastrophic but necessary reset
- Used as the foundation of law, history, and record keeping

### Scholarly View
- The Eclipse is a **temporal fracture**, not a clean break
- BE records are considered corrupted, overlapping, or symbolic
- Multiple conflicting chronologies are accepted as valid

### Mythic & Religious Views
- Some cultures interpret the Eclipse as:
  - Divine punishment
  - Elemental rebellion
  - A failed ascension
  - A mercy act to prevent total annihilation

No single interpretation is canonically privileged.

---

## 7. Known Gaps in Information (Intentional)

The following facts are **explicitly unknown or unconfirmed**:

- The **true cause** of the Eclipse
- Whether it was:
  - Triggered accidentally
  - Weaponized deliberately
  - Inevitable
- Who (if anyone) initiated it
- Whether the Infinitree existed before the Eclipse or emerged because of it
- Whether the Bonsai Sage caused, witnessed, or merely survived the event

These gaps are deliberate and protected.

---

## 8. Known Conflicts & Contradictions

### Temporal Ordering Conflicts
- Some sources place the Bonsai Sage **before** the Infinitree’s rooting
- Others state the Infinitree stabilized the Sage’s arrival
- Both are considered simultaneously true

### Elemental Records
- Fire-aligned cultures claim the Fire Emperor’s fall caused the Eclipse
- Other records claim the Eclipse caused the Fire Emperor’s fall

### Regional Memory Conflicts
- Certain regions remember **no Eclipse at all**, only its aftermath
- Others experience it as a recurring echo

These contradictions are not errors; they are manifestations of temporal damage.

---

## 9. Stability Doctrine

A derived systemic belief governs all temporal mechanics:

> **Stability is achieved through care, not control.**

This doctrine explains:
- Why Chronogardening heals rather than rewinds
- Why anomalies are resolved, not erased
- Why loops end through understanding, not force

---

## 10. Canonical Boundaries

The following constraints are non-negotiable:
- The Great Eclipse **cannot be undone**
- The world **cannot return to BE**
- No ending restores pre-Eclipse normalcy
- Any entity claiming full control over time is, by definition, corrupted or false

---

## 11. Functional Summary

- **Role**: Temporal anchor, narrative fracture, systemic law
- **Nature**: Instantaneous yet eternally consequential
- **Function**: Defines limitation, not power
- **Status**: Irreversible, unresolved, foundational

The Great Eclipse is not a mystery to be solved.

It is a wound the world learned to live with.


---

## 12. Temporal Timeline Overlay (Echo-Based)

The Great Eclipse does not behave as a single point on a linear timeline. Instead, it generates **temporal echoes** that propagate unevenly forward and backward.

### Pre‑Eclipse Echoes (BE Destabilization)
- Late BE records show **anticipatory anomalies**: failed prophecies, duplicated rulers, overlapping wars.
- Some BE civilizations recorded signs of "approaching stillness" centuries early.
- Certain artifacts appear **pre-adapted** to post‑Eclipse conditions, implying retroactive influence.

### The Eclipse Instant
- Treated as a zero-duration event with infinite consequence.
- No reliable eyewitness accounts exist that describe the full moment.

### Post‑Eclipse Echoes (AE Recurrence)
- Regional re‑manifestations of Eclipse-like effects (local blackouts, elemental pauses).
- Memory echoes replay partial scenes without full causality.
- Some anomalies stabilize only after reenacting fragments of Eclipse conditions.

The timeline is therefore **layered**, not sequential.

---

## 13. Faction & Cultural Interpretation Matrix

### Elemental Orders (Pre‑Collapse Traditions)
- View the Eclipse as a **balance failure** between elements.
- Fire-aligned sects often claim sacrifice or martyrdom narratives.
- Water and Wind traditions emphasize inevitability and flow disruption.

### Scholarly Institutions
- Treat the Eclipse as a **temporal rupture event**.
- Accept contradictory records as coexisting truths.
- Focus on containment, not explanation.

### Religious & Mythic Cultures
- Interpretations include:
  - Divine punishment
  - Mercy act to halt total annihilation
  - Failed ascension of a god-emperor
- No unified doctrine exists; plural belief is normalized.

### Isolated / Anomalous Regions
- Some communities remember **no Eclipse**, only a sudden change in reality.
- Others experience recurring partial eclipses as local phenomena.

---

## 14. Systemic Ripple Effects Across Design Layers

### Narrative Layer
- History is written in **branches and footnotes**, not absolutes.
- Legends contradict archives without invalidation.

### World & Level Design
- Zones may exist in multiple temporal states.
- Environmental storytelling relies on mismatch between place and memory.

### Gameplay Systems
- Time-based mechanics favor **stabilization and care** over manipulation.
- Loops resolve through understanding conditions, not resetting outcomes.

### Player Perception
- The player is positioned as a **participant within damage**, not an external fixer.
- Mastery comes from reading instability, not overriding it.

---

## 15. Reinforced Canon Constraints (Expanded)

- The Eclipse has no single cause that can be revealed without contradiction.
- Any timeline claiming full coherence is incomplete by definition.
- Systems that promise total temporal control are narrative traps.
- Resolution is local, never global.

These constraints apply uniformly across story, mechanics, and lore.
