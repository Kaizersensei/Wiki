# The Great Eclipse — Canonical Cause and Timeline Role

## Status
**Inevitable Crisis (Pre-Game / Pre-Agency)**

The Great Eclipse occurs before the player’s story begins. It is immutable. No ending path removes or reverses it.

---

## Origin: The Technogarch Civilization

The Technogarch civilization sought a *Perfect Future*.

Believing time to be a system that could be optimized, they attempted to **manipulate and accelerate the sun–moon cycle**, synchronizing celestial motion to eliminate uncertainty, disaster, and scarcity.

Their objective was not domination, but **permanent stability**.

---

## The Failure

The Technogarch intervention did not destroy the sun or moon.

Instead, it caused a paradoxical state:

- Motion continued
- Local time progressed
- Events still unfolded

But **global renewal ceased**.

The celestial cycle became **stale** — advancing without evolving.

This desynchronization fractured causality itself.

---

## What the Great Eclipse Is

The Great Eclipse is **not darkness**.

It is:
- Progress without healing
- Time without correction
- Change without growth

A world trapped in **permanent unresolved transition**.

---

## Consequences

- Temporal anomalies and unstable regions
- Ecosystems unable to fully recover
- Fragmented histories and overlapping eras
- Emergence of time warlords and forbidden doctrines

The damage propagates unevenly, creating regions that are:
- partially stabilized
- permanently scarred
- or actively collapsing

---

## Narrative Alignment with Timelines

### Starting Timeline
"Today is a consequence of yesterday"

The Eclipse establishes that the world is already broken when the story begins.

---

### Discovery Timeline
"The Perfect Future is a collection of past choices"

The Technogarch failure mirrors the player’s realization:
Perfect outcomes cannot be engineered through control alone.

---

### Limited Agency Timeline
"Accept what you cannot change, change what you cannot accept"

The Eclipse cannot be undone.
Only its consequences can be navigated.

---

### Full Agency Timeline
"Whether you use it or not, time won’t stop for you"

Misuse of time-based power worsens Eclipse damage.
Respecting temporal limits reduces harm but never erases it.

---

## Ending Path Consistency

- **GOOD Ending**
  - The world adapts
  - Anomalies are reduced
  - Balance is preserved through restraint

- **NEUTRAL Ending**
  - Damage is contained
  - Cycles partially stabilize

- **BAD Ending**
  - Eclipse damage persists or escalates
  - Tyranny and forced control mirror the Technogarch failure

In all outcomes, the Great Eclipse remains.
