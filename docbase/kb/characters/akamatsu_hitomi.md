# Akamatsu Hitomi

## Summary
Mother of Michio. Born in Seigetsu. While searching Varthum memory crystals to recover world-memories of Hanzo, she was ambushed by Ninth Wave and imprisoned in Mirror Bastion (Varthum), a temporal loop prison. She can only project brief flame messages when the relived memory includes fire; otherwise she is fully isolated and only knows she needs rescue.

## Known facts
- Michio's mother; married to Hanzo; student of Nagamizu lineage.
- Captured by Ninth Wave in Varthum and held in Mirror Bastion (temporal loop prison).
- Flame projections are the only outward signal; tied to fire-containing memories.
